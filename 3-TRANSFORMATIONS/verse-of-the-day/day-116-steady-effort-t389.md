# Day 116 — The Small Stream (T389)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/t389-steady-effort]] · own translation from the CBETA Chinese source (CC BY-NC-SA); Mahāyāna; no English translation of T389 is held, so grounding is the Chinese source itself.
