# Day 103 — Love for Someone You Resent (AN 5.161)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/an5-161-love-the-resented]] · grounded directly in Bhikkhu Sujato's English (CC0); Pali source also CC0.
Short prose instruction, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the person who irritates you is the specific one to be kind to.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi (`lbj`) discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. The rule docs still say seven; **do not add an `lbj` line back on their strength.**

## Source (Pali, Aṅguttara Nikāya)

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an5-161-1-3]]

> *Yasmiṁ, bhikkhave, puggale āghāto jāyetha, mettā tasmiṁ puggale bhāvetabbā.* (`pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an5-161-1-3`)

**⚑ Two notes for the reviewer.** (1) Cite **AN 5.161, never AN 5.162** — the next sutta is a near-identical list spoken by **Sāriputta** and would fail the buddhavacana gate. (2) The **second person is Sujato's translation choice**, not a literal feature of the Pali, which is an impersonal gerundive. See the rail.
