**Gate-boundary note (Toh 95) — RESOLVED by Evan, 2026-07-13.** Several candidates above (the newborn declaration, the palace-gate renunciation vow, "I will end old age and death," rejecting Rudraka Rāmaputra's attainment) are the bodhisattva's own **pre-enlightenment** words. **Ruling: these are usable as buddhavacana.** The deciding test Evan set: pre-awakening words count *only when they are from the Buddha's final lifetime — the one in which he became the Buddha — not from a past-life Jātaka.* The Lalitavistara is exactly that: a biography of Gautama's last life (Tuṣita descent → birth as Siddhārtha → renunciation → awakening → first sermon), not a Jātaka, so its pre-enlightenment lines qualify. The gate is unchanged for its normal cases — words of disciples, gods, kings, omens (e.g. the rejected "sound of the cymbals" lead), or the Bodhisattva in a *past* life still fail. Individual candidates covered by this ruling: the newborn declaration, the palace-gate vow ("until I attain unsurpassed awakening, I will not enter Kapilavastu"), "I will put an end to old age and death," rejecting Rudraka Rāmaputra's attainment, the jhāna-memory/middle-way-discovery scene (needs heavy compression), and the Tathāgata's vow not to pass into parinirvāṇa prematurely. ## Added 2026-10 — first real scan of the mid-size Kangyur band

**Method and coverage.** `SCAN-STATUS.md` had the ~229 individual Kangyur sūtra files listed as unscanned since the vault began. This pass read the **12–27 kB `en-toh*-84000.md` band in full (46 files)** and filter-screened the **27–45 kB band (21 files)**, locating candidates in the 84000 English and then verifying each survivor verbatim in the Degé Tibetan with its block anchor. The **sub-12 kB band is now fully exhausted** (its last three unexamined files — Toh 250, 309, 27 — all fail). Files that yielded nothing are listed in `rejected.md`.

**Yield: three candidates from 67 files.** The limiting factor is not length or licensing but the **self-applying and warmth screens**: Kangyur Mahāyāna prose is overwhelmingly third-person doctrinal exposition, name-recitation merit, or framed narrative. The only places it reliably speaks *to* the reader warmly are (a) protection/fearlessness sūtras, (b) Buddha-prescribed aspiration formulas, and (c) the *anumodanā* blessing clusters already mined (Toh 292/312/313/95). **Recommendation for the next Tibetan pass: grep those three shapes directly across all 229 files** rather than continuing by file size, which is now returning about one candidate per twenty files.
