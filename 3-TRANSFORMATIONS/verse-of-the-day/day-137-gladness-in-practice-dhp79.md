# Day 137 — Gladness in Practice (Dhp 79)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/dhp-79]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
One verse, **quoted in full**. Meaning: taking joy in the teaching, you live at ease — practice as gladness, not duty.

> **⚑ "the teaching" is named on purpose, and it is the one thing to watch.** The card's subject is joy in the Dhamma, so the word can't be dropped the way "Buddha" is. It ships as "the teaching" (plain), and the source's epithet "proclaimed by the Noble One" is dropped so the line stays about the reader's gladness. A reviewer should confirm it reads as gladness, not devotion.

> **⚑ "lives at ease," not "sleeps at ease."** `sukhaṁ seti` is literally "sleeps happily," but several cards already use sleep-at-ease (Days 101, 106); the point here is settled gladness, so it ships as "lives/rests at ease."

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line.**
