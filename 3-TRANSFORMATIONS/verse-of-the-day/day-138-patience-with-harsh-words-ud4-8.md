# Day 138 — Patience with Harsh Words (Ud 4.8)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/ud-4-8]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
One sentence (the verse's second sentence), **quoted in full**. Meaning: when someone speaks harshly to you, receive it with a heart that does not turn to anger.

> **⚑ The card ships the verse's SECOND sentence only — a rule-compliant cut.** The full verse opens with an elephant-and-darts battle image (padas 1–2), too martial and obscure for a daily card. Padas 3–4 are a complete sentence and carry the teaching. **Do not restore the elephant simile.** Full verse is in the rail.

> **Not "don't feel it" — receive it without the heart turning to anger.** `adhivāsana` ships as "meet / bear," not "restraint" (which reads as self-denial).

> **Pairs with Day 136 (kind speech), spaced by a day.** 136 is how you speak; this is how you take a harsh word. Kept one day apart so they don't cluster.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line.**
