# Day 122 — Nothing Left for Fear to Stand On (Toh 269)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/toh269-fearlessness]] · own translation from the Degé Kangyur (Public Domain); 84000 English as reference only; Mahāyāna.
Short verse, **quoted in full**. Meaning: hold no one beneath you and fear has no ground to stand on.

> **A genuinely new theme.** *Freedom from fear* is not on `theme-checklist.md`'s covered list at all. Days 16 and 29 describe fearlessness as a state already attained; this names an action you can take today and states the causal link.

> **⚑ 84000 emends the Degé here — the fourth confirmed case.** Its note n1 admits it prints `འཇིགས་པ` following six other editions, while Degé reads `འཇིགས་པར`. We ground on Degé. Sense identical; recorded because the pattern now holds across Toh 329, 313, 269 and a clean check on 292.

### bo ⚑
- Back-translation (approx, **against the verbatim source** per rule 8): "Always regard all beings as equal to you. Do not look down on anyone. Then fear cannot reach you." All four lines of the Degé map across; nothing added, nothing dropped. Note the rendering makes the causal link explicit with `དེ་ན` ("then"), which the source leaves to juxtaposition — flagged below.
- Findings: **our own modern Tibetan rendering**, not the verbatim Degé. Classical→modern substitutions, each checked: verse breaks `། །` flattened to prose; `རྟག་ཏུ` → the more colloquial `རྒྱུན་དུ`; the classical imperative `སེམས་བྱོས་ལ` → the modern `བསམ་བློ་འཁྱེར`; the negative imperative `མ་བརྙས` → the modern `བརྙས་བཅོས་མ་བྱེད`; `འཇིགས་པར་འབྱུང་མི་འགྱུར` → the modern `འཇིགས་པ་སླེབས་མི་སྲིད`.
- Confidence flags: (i) **`དེ་ན` is an addition** — the source juxtaposes the instruction and the result without an explicit connective. Confirm whether making the causality explicit is right for a modern reader, or whether it over-determines the verse. (ii) `བསམ་བློ་འཁྱེར` is idiomatic modern Tibetan but may be too casual for a card; a reviewer may prefer `བསམ` or `བློ་འཁྱེར`. (iii) `སླེབས་མི་སྲིད` ("cannot arrive") is stronger than `མི་འགྱུར` ("will not become") — confirm the strengthening is acceptable. (iv) `བརྙས་བཅོས` — confirm it is the right modern compound for contempt. (v) **Register: does this read as genuinely modern, or has it landed in a half-classical middle register?** This is the question to answer explicitly, and it matters more here because the source is a Kangyur verse a Tibetan reader may know.
