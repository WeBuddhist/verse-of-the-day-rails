# Day 114 — A Mind Nothing Shakes (Snp 2.4)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/snp-2-4-unshaken]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0), directly aligned at the same block anchors.
Short verse, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the world still lands on you, and it no longer knocks you over.

> **⚑ The Pali is impersonal and this card ships "your mind."** `cittaṁ yassa na kampati` is a relative clause and even Sujato renders it "**their** mind does not tremble." The second person is entirely ours, justified by the blessing genre. See the rail; a native reviewer should weigh it.

No em dash ✓. No "Buddha" in the card ✓.

### hi

- Findings: मंगल is the new termbase row for `maṅgala` ✓ and is the natural Hindi cognate. उतार-चढ़ाव is the ordinary phrase for the world's ups and downs ✓. निर्मल for `viraja` keeps the dust image ✓. ⚑ **निर्भय renders `khema` as "fearless" rather than "safe"** — a small shift from the rail (safety is a state, fearlessness a response). सुरक्षित would be literal but reads bureaucratic in Hindi.
