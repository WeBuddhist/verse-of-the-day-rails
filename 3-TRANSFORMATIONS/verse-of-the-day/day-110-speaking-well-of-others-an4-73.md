## Source (Pali, Aṅguttara Nikāya)

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an4-73-6-1]]

> *Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, sappuriso yo hoti parassa vaṇṇo taṁ apuṭṭhopi pātu karoti.* (`pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an4-73-6-1`)
