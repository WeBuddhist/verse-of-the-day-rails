# Card types — the kinds of quote, and how to distribute them

**Created 2026-08-03, at Evan's direction.** He referred to *"a list of different kinds of quotes I think"* — **there wasn't one.** Types had been implicit in the cards for 107 days and nowhere written down, which is why a whole batch of proposals came back as *"all the new quotes you supplied are similar. We want variety."* This file is that list, plus the distribution rule.

Read this alongside [`selection-criteria.md`](selection-criteria.md) (which gates *whether* a verse qualifies) and the screens in [`log.md`](log.md)'s running balance (which gate *how* it must read). **This file governs the mix.**

---

## The distribution rule (Evan, 2026-08-03)

> *"We don't want to catch up, just evenly distribute going forward."*
> *"The quotes I didn't flag are good, but just need to be spaced between the other types of quotes. If I added them sequentially as is, it would get boring."*

1. **Forward-only.** Do **not** retro-balance the back catalogue. Days 1–107 are what they are; the mix applies from Day 108 on.
2. **No two consecutive cards of the same type.** This is the same shape as the existing no-consecutive-theme rule and sits alongside it.
3. **Aim for an even spread across a rolling stretch**, not within a single batch. A six-card batch cannot contain all eight types; what matters is that no type dominates and none disappears for long.
4. **A batch of approved candidates is not a shipping order.** Six good verses of two types is a boring week. **Sequence by type, and hold candidates back rather than clumping them** — better to bank a second blessing for a later batch than run two in one stretch.
5. **When proposing, label each candidate's type.** A proposal that is type-blind will produce the clumping problem again.

## The types

### 1. Teaching / instruction
Tells the reader what to do. The default register and the largest group.
*Day 88 (hands washing) · Day 98 (keep company with one who points out your faults) · Day 105 (trouble no living being)*

### 2. Observation / how-things-work
States how something works and lets the reader draw the consequence. No imperative.
*Day 99 (doing good has a good result) · Day 96 (you become the company you keep)*

### 3. Encouragement / permission
Lowers a bar or removes a fear. Says *you may*, or *it is less than you think*.
*Day 90 (you cannot reach the path with a body worn down) · Day 101 (joy springs up when you have nothing to regret)*

### 4. Consolation
Meets a hard state and offers relief in it.
*Day 92 (whether walking or standing… you come to peace) · Day 106 (let go of anger and you sleep at ease)*

### 5. Reframe / paradox
Turns an ordinary judgement over. The card's force is the surprise.
*Day 98 (correction is buried treasure) · Day 93 (gratitude shows in the small kindness, not the large)*

### 6. Simile / image
Carries the point through a picture. **Highest-risk type** — see the self-applying screen; a simile only works when the quote names its own referent.
*Day 88 (two hands) · Day 97 (merit unbroken by wind, fire, flood)*

### 7. Blessing — **new 2026-08-03**
The Buddha wishes the reader well. The reader *receives*.
*Day 104 (may your night be well, your day be well)*

### 8. Aspiration / wish — **new 2026-08-03**
A wish the reader *makes*. Includes dedication of merit.
*Day 107 (may all beings find only happiness)*

**Types 7 and 8 were added by Evan's genre ruling:** *"The problem before was the buddha praising himself. I don't want to include those, but when they are blessings and aspirations, they are good."* They are additions to this list, **not a replacement for it** — see the supply note below.

## ⚑ Supply constraint on types 7 and 8 — they cannot carry the calendar

When the genre opened, the obvious question was whether the calendar could run on blessings. **It cannot, and the reason is supply, established by corpus-wide sweeps (2026-08-03):**

- **Tibetan:** all 229 `bo-toh*.md` files swept for `ཤོག` / `གྱུར་ཅིག` / `བདེ་ལེགས` / `བཀྲ་ཤིས` and for `བསོད་ནམས` + optative. ~45 files carry aspiration verses, but **nearly all are spoken by bodhisattvas, gods, kings, or the reader** — gate 1 failures. A frame sweep for the *anumodanā* formula returns **only Toh 95 and Toh 292** as Buddha-spoken benediction clusters. Add Toh 312, Toh 182 and Toh 313 and the Kangyur yields roughly **eight usable verses**.
- **Pali:** a dedicated blessing sweep returned 805 hits on *may / auspicious / prosper / be well / long life*, of which essentially **AN 3.155 and SN 10.4** survive the speaker gate.

**Total: ten to twelve verses in the entire corpus.** At one a day that is under two weeks before repetition or a gate breach. **Import lead if more is ever wanted:** 84000's note on Toh 312 records **Toh 816, "The Verses of Well-Wishing"** — a standalone Kangyur text consisting entirely of this genre, not currently in `1-SOURCES/`.

**And the brand rule points the same way.** `selection-criteria.md`'s brand grounding quotes the North Star: *"Sacred, Not Precious — it should feel like home, not like a wellness app,"* with the platform rule that every session carries a verified canonical quote. **An all-affirmation calendar is the wellness-app failure mode that line exists to prevent.** Part of why Days 104 and 107 land is that they are unexpected against a hundred teachings.

## Two content screens added the same day (Evan, 2026-08-03)

These are **gates, not preferences**, and both came from rejections in the Days 108–113 proposal.

### ⚑ A. The card must be dharma, not general life advice
**Rejected: AN 2.33**, *"Parents give their children so much: they raise them, nurture them, and show them the world."* Evan: **"D doesn't work because it's just about parenting. Not so much about dharma."**

The verse is authentic buddhavacana, warm, self-applying, and passes every other screen. It fails because **its subject is not the practice.** True, kind, and secular is not enough.

- **This also finally settles the `gratitude-to-parents` theme**, which the log had recorded as a dead end for the wrong reason. Toh 315 and Iti 106 were rejected over the Brahmā framing, which left the impression that a deity-free parents verse would work. **It would not — the topic itself is off-dharma.** Stop looking for one, and treat the theme as closed rather than blocked.
- **Practical test:** could this sentence appear in a secular book of life advice with nothing lost? If yes, it is not a card. Note this screen and the wellness-app brand rule are the same concern from two directions.

### ⚑ B. The word "Buddha" should not appear in the card
**Rejected: Toh 312**, *"By the merit of the Buddha and the good wishes of the gods, may whatever you hope for be accomplished this very day."* Evan: **"H doesn't work because it has the word buddha in it."**

Taken as stated: **the shipped card text should not name the Buddha.** The card is something the reader receives for their own life; naming him turns it devotional, or makes the card about him rather than about them. (In H's case he is also the *agent* of the blessing, which compounds it.) This is consistent with the earlier rejection of Toh 293's *"remember the Buddha, the guide of the world"* for Day 95 — the reason is now explicit.

## How to use this file

- **Selecting:** tag every candidate with its type. Check the last few days' types before sequencing.
- **Proposing:** show the type column. A proposal of eight candidates in two types will be sent back.
- **Building:** the day card needs no `type:` field yet — but if the mix is to be tracked mechanically, adding one to the card frontmatter and the `log.md` table is the obvious next step, and worth doing before the calendar gets much longer.
- **Growing this list:** a genuinely new form (as blessings and aspirations were) gets added here with the ruling that admitted it. Don't invent types for one-off variations in tone.
