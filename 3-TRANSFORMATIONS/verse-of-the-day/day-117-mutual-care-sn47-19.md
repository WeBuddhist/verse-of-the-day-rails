# Day 117 — Looking After Yourself (SN 47.19)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/sn47-19-mutual-care]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0), directly aligned at the same block anchor.
Short sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the two directions are not a trade-off, they make each other.

> **⚑ The Pali is impersonal; the second person is Sujato's, not ours.** `rakkhanto … rakkhati` has no pronoun anywhere. Sujato supplies "you" twice, and that choice is what lets this card pass the self-applying screen — the standing Nikāya finding, same as Day 103. Weaker case than Day 114, where the second person was ours.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**
