# Day 132 — Contentment (Dhp 204)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/dhp-204]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
One stanza, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the real riches are things you can actually have — being well, being content, being trusted, being at peace.

> **⚑ A list is fine — this corrects an over-broad rejection (Evan, 2026-08-31).** Dhp 204 was on `rejected.md` as "presumed dead" under the "one idea, one image" rule. Evan's ruling: *"There's nothing wrong with a list. That's different than a bunch of mixed ideas in one quote."* Dhp 204 is a **list** — four "the highest X" claims under one frame (what is the best of each kind of good?) — not the mixed, frameless stack the rule targets (Day 87's faith/practice/truth/wisdom). It has never shipped. **See the rail: the rejected.md and log entries that swept in Dhp 204, Snp 3.3 and Iti 93 should be re-scoped by a human — those may be valid unified lists too.**

> **The frame is what makes it read fast.** "The highest X, the highest Y" repeats, so the reader holds one pattern four times, not four unrelated ideas. Keep the parallel structure in every rendering — it is what unifies the list.

> **⚑⚑ The one decision for the reviewer: `nibbāna` ships as "peace," not named.** The fourth pada cannot be dropped (kept-whole), and the card avoids naming a doctrinal term as its subject, so `nibbāna` is rendered plainly as "peace." This under-translates the highest term on purpose, to hold the card at one plain register. A reviewer may object either way. **Do not silently restore "Nibbāna" or "extinguishment."**

> **⚑ "a trusted friend" carries `vissāsa` (trust) in a person** — `vissāsa` is trust, `ñāti` is kin. "A trusted friend is the closest kin" keeps both nouns. "friend" is supplied, not invented; flagged.
