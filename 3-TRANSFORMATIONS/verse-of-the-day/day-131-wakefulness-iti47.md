# Day 131 — Wakefulness (Iti 47)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/iti-47]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
One quatrain, **quoted in full** (with the two-part address compressed, see below). Meaning: come awake. Being awake beats sleeping through your life, and there is nothing in it to fear.

> **Passes the one-second screen cleanly.** No image to decode, no clause to invert, no context needed. Rouse yourself, it is better, and it is safe. This is why it survived the proposal where Snp 3.11 and the others did not.

> **The literal surface is kept on purpose.** `jāgara` is spiritual wakefulness (`appamāda`-adjacent), but a reader who hears "wake up" plainly gets a true, usable meaning at once, and the deeper sense (stop drifting through your days) arrives on its own. Unlike a verse that needs its backstory, here the backstory only deepens what already lands.

> **⚑ The two-part address is compressed to one.** The Pali says "you who are awake, listen" and then "you who sleep, wake up." On a card the reader is one person, so the shipped line keeps only the move that speaks to them: "wake up." This drops `suṇātha` ("listen"). Flagged for the reviewer — the claim (`seyyo`) and the reassurance (`natthi bhayaṁ`) are both kept whole.

> **⚑ Ends on reassurance, not on the problem.** The only heavy word, `bhaya` (fear), appears negated: "nothing to fear." Keep that arc in every rendering — the card must not close on the fear.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**
