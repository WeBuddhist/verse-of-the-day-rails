# Day 89 — Detachment from Gain and Honour (Udānavarga 13)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/udv-gain-and-honor]] · own translation from the Degé Kangyur (Public Domain), grounded on the Pali parallel Dhp 365 (Sujato, CC0) — lines 2 to 4 exact, line 1 divergent.
Short verse, **quoted in full**. Meaning: wanting someone else's share is not just unattractive, it is what keeps the mind from settling.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi (`lbj`) discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. The rule docs still say seven (the decision was "stop going forward only"); **do not add an `lbj` line back to this card on the strength of them.**

## Source (Tibetan, Udānavarga — verbatim)

> རང་གི་རྙེད་པ་བླང་བྱ་ཞིང་། །གཞན་གྱི་ལ་ནི་འདོད་མི་སྐྱེད། །དགེ་སློང་གཞན་གྱི་འདོད་བྱེད་པས། །ཏིང་འཛིན་ཐོབ་པར་མི་འགྱུར་རོ། (`bo-udanavarga.md#^p442`)

Pali parallel (Dhp 365, `pi-dhammapada.md#^365`): *Salābhaṁ nātimaññeyya, nāññesaṁ pihayaṁ care; Aññesaṁ pihayaṁ bhikkhu, samādhiṁ nādhigacchati.*
