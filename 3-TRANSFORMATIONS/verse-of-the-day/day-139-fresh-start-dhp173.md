# Day 139 — Fresh Start (Dhp 173)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/dhp-173]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
One verse, **quoted in full**. Meaning: a past wrong doesn't define you — the good you do now outshines it, the way the moon comes clear of cloud.

> **Outshone, not erased.** The wrong happened; the point is that present good is brighter, not that consequences vanish. Keep that honest sense in every rendering.

> **The moon image is the whole card — keep it concrete.** A familiar, everyday sight (the opposite of the chariot-linchpin problem). It names its own referent: the person turning to good *is* the moon emerging.

> **Not bleak.** "A past wrong" opens it, but the arc moves straight to good and ends on light. Close on the moon, not the wrong.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line.**

## Source (Pali, Dhammapada 173 — verbatim)
![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-dhammapada.md#^173]]
