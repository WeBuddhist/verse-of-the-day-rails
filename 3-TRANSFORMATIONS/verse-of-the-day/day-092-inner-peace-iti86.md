# Day 92 — Inner Peace (Iti 86)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/iti-86]] · grounded directly in Bhikkhu Sujato's aligned English (CC0); Pali source also CC0.
Short verse, **quoted in full**. Meaning: peace does not wait on your circumstances or your posture, only on one inward thing.

## Renderings (one short line each)

- **en** — Whether walking or standing, sitting or lying down, with the mind settled within, you come to peace.
- **zh** — 不論是行走、站立、坐著或躺著，只要心在裡面安定下來，你就會得到安穩。
- **bo** — ⚑ འགྲོ་བ་ཡིན་ནའང་། ལངས་པ་ཡིན་ནའང་། བསྡད་པ་ཡིན་ནའང་། ཉལ་བ་ཡིན་ནའང་། སེམས་ནང་ནས་ཞི་འཇགས་བྱུང་ན། ཞི་བདེ་ཐོབ་ཀྱི་རེད།
- **hi** — चलते हुए या खड़े, बैठे या लेटे, जब मन भीतर से शांत हो जाता है, तब शांति मिल जाती है।
- **ne** — हिँड्दै होस् वा उभिँदै, बस्दै होस् वा सुत्दै, मन भित्रबाट शान्त भएपछि शान्ति प्राप्त हुन्छ।

## Why this card was selected

Recorded because the last three batches lost five cards to tone and clarity, and this one was chosen directly against those failures. It is **self-applying** (no person, place, scene or event — it addresses anyone and the meaning is entirely on the surface); **one idea** (the four postures are a single idiom, not four claims, unlike the Day 87 rejection); and **warm rather than corrective** (unlike the Day 89 rejection). Worth noting *why* it works: **not** because it is vivid — it has no image at all — but because a reader knows immediately what it is saying to them. That is the screen that now matters most.
