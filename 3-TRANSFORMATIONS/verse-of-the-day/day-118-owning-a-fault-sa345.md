# Day 118 — Seeing It Yourself (SĀ 345)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/sa-345-owning-a-fault]] · own translation from the CBETA Chinese source (CC BY-NC-SA), grounded on the Pali parallel SN 12.70 (Sujato, CC0). No Patton translation exists for SĀ 345.
Short sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the honest look is not the end of something, it is the start of a growth that does not reverse.

> **⚑ The Pali parallel's surroundings are harsh and are deliberately excluded.** SN 12.70 runs an extended simile of a bandit bound, paraded and beheaded, and speaks of the underworld, immediately before the sentence we ground on. The Chinese at this anchor contains none of it. **Do not pull it in for completeness** — it would fail the warmth screen outright. The confession formula the Buddha gives Susīma to recite is excluded for the same reason.

## Source (Chinese, SĀ 345 — verbatim)

Full passage in the block:
> 佛告須深：「受汝悔過，汝當具說：『我昔愚癡、不善、無智，於正法、律盜密出家，今日悔過，自見罪、自知罪，於當來世律儀成就，功德增長，終不退減。』所以者何？凡人有罪，自見、自知而悔過者，於當來世律儀成就，功德增長，終不退減。」 (`zh-samyukta-agama.md#^pT02p0097c1714`)

The shipped sentence, verbatim from it:
> 凡人有罪，自見、自知而悔過者，於當來世律儀成就，功德增長，終不退減。

### en
- Back-translation: n/a (anchor rendering).
- Findings: all four elements present — the general case, the self-seeing plus owning, the settled conduct, the growth that does not reverse ✓. **`律儀成就` ships as "becomes steadier"** per the rail flag: "restraint" reads as self-denial to a lay reader and "the discipline" reads monastic, and neither passes the no-glossary screen. **`功德` ships as "goodness," not "merit"** per the rail flag — deliberately keeping the card off the merit ground that Days 97, 100, 108 and 111 already hold. **`罪` ships as "fault," not "sin" (theistic) or "crime" (legal).** The doubled `自` is carried by "their own… and owns it" ✓. `終不退減` keeps its emphatic finality in "never falls back" ✓. The Pali parallel's fourth element, "commit to restraint in the future," is **not** imported — the card follows the Chinese structure ✓. 112 chars. No em dash ✓. No "Buddha" in the card ✓.
- Net: clean for review.

### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation (against the verbatim source, per rule 8): "When a person has done wrong, if they can see it themselves, admit it themselves and change, then afterwards their conduct will be firmer, their goodness will grow, and it will not fall away." Maps onto 凡人有罪，自見、自知而悔過者，於當來世律儀成就，功德增長，終不退減 with no drift and nothing added.
- Findings: **our modern rendering, not the CBETA text.** Substitutions, each checked: `凡人有罪` → `一個人有了過失`; `自見、自知而悔過者` → `能自己看見、自己承認並改過`; `於當來世` → `日後`; `律儀成就` → `行為會更穩固`; `功德增長` → `善德會增長`; `終不退減` → `不會退失`. ⚑ **`功德` is deliberately not carried through as 功德** — 善德 is used instead, for the same reason the English avoids "merit". A reviewer may prefer to restore 功德 on the Chinese line, where the term is less loaded than in English; flagged as a live choice. 41 chars. Taiwan/HK/SG register ✓, Traditional ✓, genuinely modern ✓.
- Net: clean for review, with the 功德 / 善德 choice flagged.
