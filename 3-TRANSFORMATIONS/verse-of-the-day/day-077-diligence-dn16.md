# Day 77 — Diligence (DN 16)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/dn16-last-words]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
Short sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: everything assembled comes apart, so do not waste the time you have. The Buddha's last words.

## Source (Pali)

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-digha-nikaya.md#^dn16-6-7-3]]

## Renderings (one short line each)

- **en** — Conditioned things are bound to fall away. Carry the work through with diligence.
- **zh** — 一切因緣和合之法終將壞滅，當以不放逸將修行貫徹到底。
- **bo** — ⚑ འདུ་བྱེད་རྣམས་འཇིག་པའི་ཆོས་ཅན་ཡིན། བག་ཡོད་པས་མཐར་ཕྱིན་པར་གྱིས།
- **hi** — सभी संस्कार विनाशशील हैं। अप्रमाद के साथ साधना को पूरा करो।
- **ne** — सबै संस्कार विनाशशील छन्। अप्रमादपूर्वक साधनालाई पूरा गर।
- **mn** — ⚑ Хуран үйлдэгдсэн бүхэн эвдрэх чанартай. Сэрэмжтэйгээр номоо бүтээж дуусга.
- **lbj** — ⚑ दुज्येत् थम्चत् जिक्पी छोस्चन् यिन्। बग्योत् च्यास्ते मथर् स्क्योल्।

## QA — pre-review (against 2-RAILS/Verses/dn16-last-words.md)

### en
- Back-translation: "Conditioned things are bound to fall away. Carry the work through with diligence."
- Findings: both clauses of the rail present. *vayadhammā* rendered as "bound to fall away" (the rail's "of a nature to decay"), *sampādetha* as "carry the work through" per flag (b), which keeps the completion sense Sujato's "persist" leaves implicit. *appamādena* = "with diligence," the termbase rendering, not "carefully." Rendered inclusively with no "monks" per flag (d). Not stitched: this is the whole of `^dn16-6-7-3`. ~81 chars. No em dash ✓.
- Findings (tone): sober subject, but the sentence's own second clause carries the way through, so it passes `selection-criteria.md` §2 rather than needing framing added.
- Net: clean for review.

### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation: "All things formed by the coming together of conditions will in the end break up; carry practice through to the end with diligence."
- Findings: our modern Traditional rendering (source is Pali). 因緣和合之法 for *saṅkhārā* per flag (a) — plainer than the technical 諸行, which a Tier 3/4 reader may not parse. 不放逸 is the termbase rendering of *appamāda* ✓. 貫徹到底 carries *sampādetha*'s completion sense. Taiwan/HK/SG register.
- Net: clean for review.

### hi

- Findings: संस्कार is the standard Hindi Buddhist term for *saṅkhārā* per flag (a); अप्रमाद is the termbase rendering ✓; पूरा करो carries *sampādetha*. Faithful, nothing added.
- Net: clean for review.

### bo ⚑
- Back-translation (approx): "Conditioned formations are of a nature to perish. With diligence, bring it to completion."
- Confidence flags: our rendering (source is Pali, not Tibetan). འདུ་བྱེད་རྣམས chosen for *saṅkhārā* for consistency with the Day 19 Udānavarga card (`udv-impermanence`, which reads འདུ་བྱེད་རྣམས་མི་རྟག); བག་ཡོད is the termbase rendering of *appamāda* ✓. Confirm མཐར་ཕྱིན་པར་གྱིས is the right imperative for *sampādetha* (alternatives: བརྩོན་པར་གྱིས "strive," གྲུབ་པར་བྱོས "accomplish") — the completion sense matters here.
- Net: escalate to native Tibetan dharma reviewer.

### lbj (Ladakhi) ⚑
- Back-translation (approx): "All conditioned things are of a nature to perish. Acting with diligence, carry it through to the end."
- Findings: **Devanagari, per Evan's 2026-09 correction** — an earlier draft of this card wrongly rendered `lbj` in Tibetan script, which made it a near-copy of the `bo` line; that has been redone. Ladakhi content words in Devanagari: `दुज्येत्` (dujyet, the Ladakhi reflex of `འདུ་བྱེད`, *saṅkhārā*), `जिक्पी छोस्चन्` (jikpi chöchan, of a perishable nature), `बग्योत्` (bagyot, `བག་ཡོད`, diligence), `मथर् स्क्योल्` (mathar kyol, carry to completion). **Not** the `hi` line, which uses the Sanskrit-derived संस्कार and अप्रमाद — this uses the Tibetic terms instead.
- Confidence flags — **lowest confidence of any language on this card, and the script correction adds a second layer of uncertainty.** (i) **Orthography is unresolved:** nothing in this vault fixes which Devanagari convention for Ladakhi we use, so every spelling above is a proposal, not a standard — settling that convention is the reviewer's first job and it may change all of these. (ii) Confirm the word choices are the ones Ladakhi speakers actually use, rather than Tibetan forms mechanically transcribed. (iii) Confirm nothing has drifted into Hindi: the script is shared with `hi`, the language is not. (iv) Check the verbal endings and case particles, the layer least likely to survive transcription from Tibetan. Treat this line as a draft to rewrite, not to approve.
- Net: escalate to native Ladakhi dharma reviewer.
