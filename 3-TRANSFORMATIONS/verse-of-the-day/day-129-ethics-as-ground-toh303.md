# Day 129 — Ethics as Ground (Toh 303)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/toh303-vase-and-ethics]] · own translation from the Degé Kangyur (Public Domain); 84000 English as reference only.
One couplet, **quoted in full**. Meaning: ethical conduct is not the constraint on a good life, it is the container that lets one hold.

> **The simile names its own referent.** The second half says what the vase stands for, so the reader is never left to work out the comparison. That is why this clears the simile screen that `card-types.md` calls the highest-risk type.

> **⚑ The one way this card can go wrong: `ཆོས་རྣམས` means "qualities," not "the Dharma."** `ཆོས` carries both senses. Heard the second way, the line claims ethics produces *the teaching itself*, which is a different and much larger claim. 84000 reads "good qualities," and the neighbouring padas pair `ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས` with reputation, radiance and being praised, which are personal qualities. **Every rendering below disambiguates toward "qualities." Do not let a reviewer "restore" the ambiguity.**

> **⚑ "ethical conduct," not "moral discipline."** 84000's term reads punitive and monastic to a lay reader, the same objection the termbase records for "restraint" in the `saṃvara` row. The termbase `sīla` row permits both; the card takes the warmer end.

> **⚑ Do not extend the quote.** The very next pada is this image's negative twin (`ཇི་ལྟར་བུམ་ཆག...` — a broken vase holds nothing, and shattered conduct loses every quality). Pulling it in turns an affirmative card into a warning.

> **⚑ This entry was held back as borderline and offered anyway.** `candidate-pool/tibetan.md` flagged it on warmth: it is the one salvageable couplet in a text that is otherwise a wall of "discipline is the best ornament / the best fragrance," and the austere surroundings may make it read as moralising by association. Offered to Evan with the flag stated and selected 2026-08-31, per the Day 122 precedent. **If it reads as moralising in review, this is why.**

> **⚑ Adjacency to Day 130, deliberately not resolved here.** Day 130 (Iti 59) also leads with ethics and also closes on an image. Evan approved both in one turn with the adjacency stated, and in the same turn said the calendar has too many ethics topics. **Recommended fix: slot a non-ethics card between them and move Iti 59 to Day 131.** Flagged rather than renumbered unasked.
