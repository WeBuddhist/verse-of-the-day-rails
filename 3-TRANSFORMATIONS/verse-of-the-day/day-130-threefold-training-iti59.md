# Day 130 — The Three, Developed Together (Iti 59)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/iti-59]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
One quatrain, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the three trainings are not a menu. What carries you past what pulls at you is having developed all three, and developed them well.

> **The verse opens the teaching out, and the card ships the verse for that reason.** The prose just above it (`^iti59-2-3`) predicates these three of a *bhikkhu*, and of the *adept* (`asekha`) at that. The verse drops both and puts `yassa` in their place: "whoever." That is the form in which this is addressed to anyone, so it is the form that ships.

> **⚑⚑ Māra is the open question on this card, and it is the curator's, not the reviewer's.** Three things to weigh, set out in full in the rail: (1) Māra is **not** a Brahmā-class problem — the screen that killed Toh 315 is about culture-specific *deity or Vedic* frames, and Māra is specifically Buddhist, present under his own name in all three canons (`བདུད`, 魔), so he is recognisable across the whole audience rather than to one part of it. (2) He is still **a named mythological figure**, and the standing screen is that a card must land in one second; a reader who does not know the name gets "something is escaped," which is most of the meaning but not all of it. (3) The alternative — rendering the sense as "beyond temptation's reach" — **drops a term the tradition names**, which is the paraphrase the ecumenical-wording rule exists to prevent. A `māra` termbase row has been added rather than deciding this per-verse.

> **⚑ `samādhi` ships as "stillness of mind," not "concentration."** "Concentration" reads as effortful mental strain, close to the opposite of what is meant; "immersion" is Sujato's idiolect and not current elsewhere. This takes the **second** option already in the termbase `samādhi` row, so it is inside the row, not a departure. ⚑ The `santi` row's caution runs the other way and is respected: "stillness" must **not** leak back into peace contexts as a rendering of `santi`.

> **⚑ Card type is `teaching` though the line closes on a simile.** The sentence's work is the instruction; the sun is its last clause, not its structure. This matters because Day 129 is a `simile` card and two of a type cannot run back to back (`card-types.md` rule 2). **The label is honest rather than convenient — a reviewer who disagrees should reorder these two, not relabel this one.**

> **⚑ Adjacency to Day 129.** Both cards lead with ethics. Evan approved both in one turn with the adjacency stated, and in the same turn said the calendar has too many ethics topics. **Recommended fix: slot a non-ethics card between them, moving this card to Day 131.** Flagged rather than renumbered unasked.

### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation: "A person who has practised all three of 戒, 定, 慧 to maturity has already walked out of Māra's range, bright like the sun."
- Findings: **戒 / 定 / 慧 are the termbase terms** and are the compact set every Chinese-speaking Buddhist reader knows on sight ✓ — this is the one language where the threefold formula needs no unpacking at all. **魔 is the standard Chinese term** and needs no gloss ✓. 修習純熟 for `subhāvitā` keeps "well" as maturity rather than mere completion ✓. 走出…範圍 for `atikkamma māradheyyaṁ` ✓. ⚑ 光明 was chosen over 照耀 ("shines upon") because the Pali `virocati` is intransitive radiance, not illumination of something else. Taiwan/HK/SG register ✓, Traditional ✓.
- Net: clean for review.
