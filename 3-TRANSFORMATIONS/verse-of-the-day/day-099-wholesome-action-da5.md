### en
- Back-translation: n/a (source language of the rail's Disambiguated Meaning).
- Findings: **`必` survives as "is sure to"** per rail flag (a) — the reliability *is* the teaching, and dropping it would leave a vague encouragement. **"Result," not "reward"** per flag (b), avoiding an implied agent who grants it. **The `清白`/`白` echo is preserved** per flag (c) — "lives cleanly… a clean one" — since the resemblance between act and outcome is the second clause's point. **"Lives cleanly"** for `清白行` per flag (d), blameless and upright rather than ritually or bodily pure. **The caste argument is absent** per the rail's ⚑ flag — the source continues into "if this result belonged to brahmins alone…" and none of that is imported ✓. **The negative twin is not used** — the pool's older entry had it; this is the positive form ✓. 99 chars. ### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation: "A person who does good things will certainly have a good result; a person who lives blamelessly will certainly have a blameless result."
- Findings: **our modern Traditional rendering, not the verbatim CBETA text** (per the note above). Back-translated **against the verbatim source** as rule 8 requires: `夫行善法必有善報` → `做好事的人一定會有好的結果`; `行清白行必有白報` → `活得清白的人，一定會有清白的結果`. Classical→modern substitutions: the Literary topic particle `夫` dropped, having no modern equivalent; `行善法` → `做好事`; `必有` → `一定會有` per flag (a); `行清白行` → `活得清白`, since the classical verb-object repetition is not modern usage; **`白報` → `清白的結果`**, spelling out what the abbreviated classical `白` carries — this preserves the `清白`/`白` echo per flag (c) at the cost of the compression, which is the right trade in a modern register. Taiwan/HK/SG register.
- Net: clean for review.

### hi
- Back-translation: "One who does good certainly receives a good fruit; one who lives a pure life certainly receives a pure fruit."
- Findings: अवश्य carries `必` per flag (a). फल for `報` — "fruit," which avoids the reward/agent problem of flag (b) and matches the standard Indic idiom for karmic result. निर्मल is used for **both** the conduct and the result, preserving the `清白`/`白` echo per flag (c) — the cleanest solution of the six languages. भला for `善`, ordinary rather than technical.
- Net: clean for review.

### ne

- Net: needs native review.

### bo ⚑

- Findings: source is **Chinese**, not Tibetan, so this is a fresh rendering from the rail's meaning rather than a modernisation of a Tibetan source. `དགེ་བ` is the `termbase.md` *kusala* cell. `ངེས་པར` carries `必` per flag (a). `འབྲས་བུ` for `報` — "fruit," avoiding the reward/agent problem per flag (b). `གཙང་མ` used for **both** conduct and result, preserving the echo per flag (c).
- Confidence flags: (i) confirm `གཙང་མར་འཚོ` reads as living blamelessly rather than living hygienically — this is the same risk flagged on the Nepali line and the two should be decided together. (iii) Confirm the doubled `ཡོང་གི་རེད` ending is not repetitive across two short clauses.
