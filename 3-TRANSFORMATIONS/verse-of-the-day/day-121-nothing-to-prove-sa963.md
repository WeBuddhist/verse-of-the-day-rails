# Day 121 — Whatever You Have Is Met (SĀ 963)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/sa-963-i-will-fill-it-in]] · own translation from the CBETA Chinese source (CC BY-NC-SA), grounded on the Pali parallel AN 3.64 (Sujato, CC0), which is sentence-for-sentence. No Patton translation exists for SĀ 963.
Short sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: neither branch is failure. Whatever you have is met.

> **⚑ The Chinese and Pali order the clauses oppositely and the card follows the Chinese.** Sujato has the incomplete case first ("If you've not learned it fully, I'll fill you in"); the Chinese opens on gladness. The Chinese order is better for a card — the reader is not led in through their own shortfall. Not an error.

### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation (against the verbatim source, per rule 8): "If what you know is already complete, I am glad for you; if it is not yet complete, I will help you make it up." Maps onto 汝所知滿足者，我則隨喜；不滿足者，我當令汝滿足 clause for clause, no drift, nothing added.
- Findings: **our modern rendering, not the CBETA text.** Substitutions, each checked: `汝所知滿足者` → `你所知道的如果已經圓滿`; `我則隨喜` → `我就為你歡喜`; `不滿足者` → `如果還不圓滿`; `我當令汝滿足` → `我就幫你補足`; the classical `者…則…` conditional pair → the modern `如果…就…`. ⚑ **`隨喜` is deliberately unpacked to 為你歡喜** rather than kept: in modern Chinese bare 隨喜 has drifted toward "congratulations" and toward temple-donation contexts, which would misdirect the line. A reviewer may prefer to restore 隨喜, and this is the live choice on the Chinese line. 圓滿 rather than 滿足 for `滿足`, since modern 滿足 reads as "satisfied" — the exact shift the English also avoids ✓. 34 chars. Taiwan/HK/SG register ✓, Traditional ✓.
- Net: clean for review, with the 隨喜 / 為你歡喜 choice flagged.

### hi
- Back-translation: "If what you know is complete then I am pleased for you; and if it is not complete then I will have it completed."
- Findings: प्रसन्न carries the gladness-for-another sense without reaching for मुदिता, which would be the technical term and needs no place on a card ✓ — but note the termbase row for *muditā* is मुदिता, so this is a deliberate plain-register choice, flagged. पूरा for `滿足` keeps the fillable sense ✓. तुम्हारे लिए makes the gladness explicitly other-directed, which is the whole point of `隨喜` ✓. करा दूँगा is a warm causative future carrying `當令` ✓.
- Net: clean for review, with the प्रसन्न / मुदिता register choice flagged.
