# Day 135 — Loving-Kindness (Snp 1.8)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/snp-1-8]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
One verse, **quoted in full**. Meaning: take the fierce care a mother has for her only child and widen it, without limit, to every living being.

> **The simile sets the degree, not a limit on who.** The mother-child image measures *how much* care, then the verse extends it to all beings. Keep both halves in every rendering.

> **⚑ mettā ships per termbase — "loving-kindness / 慈 / བྱམས་པ / मैत्री," never "love."** The standard-term rule; "love" invites the affection misreading the vault has flagged before.

> **⚑ A well-known verse, chosen deliberately.** Its recognizability is a plus for a casual audience, not the over-exposure the freshness screen guards against (Evan, 2026-08-31).

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line.**

## Renderings (one short line each)

- **zh** — 就像母親用生命守護自己的獨子，也願你對一切眾生懷抱無量的慈心。
- **bo** — ⚑ མ་ཞིག་གིས་རང་གི་བུ་གཅིག་པུ་སྲོག་གིས་སྲུང་བ་ནང་བཞིན། སེམས་ཅན་ཐམས་ཅད་ལ་ཚད་མེད་པའི་བྱམས་སེམས་ཤིག་སྐྱེད།

- **ne** — जसरी आमाले आफ्नो एक्लो सन्तानलाई आफ्नो ज्यानले रक्षा गर्छिन्, त्यसरी नै सबै प्राणीहरूप्रति असीम मैत्रीको हृदय राख।
