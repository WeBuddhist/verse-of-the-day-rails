# Day 125 — Be the Friend (Toh 55)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/toh55-be-the-good-friend]] · own translation from the Degé Kangyur (Public Domain); 84000 English as reference only; Mahāyāna.
Two padas forming one imperative sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the canon usually tells you to find a good spiritual friend; this tells you to be one.

> **The reversal is the card.** Days 1, 80, 85, 96 and 117 all address the reader as the person who **has**, **finds**, or **is shaped by** good company. This is the only card on the calendar that makes the reader **the friend**, and the `ཕྱིར` clause makes the beneficiary someone else.

> **⚑⚑ The `bo` line spells out a term the source contracts, and here that is a meaning fix, not a register fix.** The source has `དགེ་བཤེས` — classically the contraction of `དགེ་བའི་བཤེས་གཉེན` (*kalyāṇamitra*, the spiritual friend). **In modern Tibetan `དགེ་བཤེས` means a Geshe, the monastic degree.** Passing the classical contraction through would tell a Tibetan reader to become a degree-holder. This is the strongest case the vault has yet had for the source-language-ships-modern rule.

> **The address is genuinely second-person in the Tibetan.** `ཁྱོད` is explicit and `རབ་ཏུ་བྱོས` is a true imperative — **no second person is supplied by a translator here**, unlike Days 114, 120 and 124. Rare in this corpus, and it means the self-applying screen is met by the source itself.

> **⚑ Day 122 is two days back and mentions friends.** That card is *giving increases your friends* — what happens to your circle. This is an instruction to be a certain kind of person for others. Flagged so the sequencing can be overruled rather than the echo discovered.

> **⚑ Note on bo:** the verbatim Degé sentence below is **the source**; the shipped `bo` is **modern, plain Tibetan** like every other language. **Do not "correct" it back.**

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**
