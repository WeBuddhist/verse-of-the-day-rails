# Day 134 — Present in Every Posture (Ud 5.10)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/ud-5-10]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
One sentence (the verse's first sentence), **quoted in full**. Meaning: presence is not only for the cushion. Carry it standing, sitting or lying down, and it grows.

> **⚑ The card ships the verse's FIRST sentence only, and this is a rule-compliant cut, not a fragment.** The full udāna is two sentences; the second adds "and passes beyond the sight of the King of Death." The vault's rule allows "one self-contained sentence, quoted in full" — padas 1 to 4 are exactly that, and cutting there keeps the death image off a daily card. **Do not extend the quote to the death line.** The full verse is in the rail for transparency.

> **The teaching is the whole point: presence is not confined to formal sitting.** It is available in any posture, across the ordinary day, and it compounds. Keep "standing, sitting, or lying down" concrete in every rendering — that is what makes it feel usable, not abstract.

> **⚑ "stay present" compresses `etaṁ satiṁ ... adhiṭṭhahāno` ("keeping to this mindfulness").** A reviewer should confirm the *sustained holding* sense survives — it is not a one-off noticing but a keeping-to.

> **⚑ "you only grow" renders `pubbāpariyaṁ visesaṁ`** (a distinction that accrues over time). A reviewer may prefer "you keep growing / grow steadily."

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**
