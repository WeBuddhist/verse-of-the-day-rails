# Day 109 — A Blessing on Coming and Going (Toh 95)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/toh95-blessing-going-returning]] · own translation from the Degé Kangyur (Public Domain); 84000 English as reference only, **footnote-checked clean**.
Short verse, **quoted in full**. Meaning: a blessing on leaving, on returning, and on both sides of the meeting at the end.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**

## Source (Tibetan, Kangyur Toh 95 — verbatim)

> ཁྱེད་ཅག་འགྲོ་ནའང་བཀྲ་ཤིས་གྱུར། །ལྡོག་པ་ན་ཡང་བཀྲ་ཤིས་ཤོག །གཉེན་ལ་ལྟ་བ་བཀྲ་ཤིས་གྱུར། །གཉེན་གྱིས་བལྟ་བའང་བཀྲ་ཤིས་ཤོག (`bo-toh95.md#^p375`)

Spoken by the Buddha to **Trapuṣa and Bhallika**, his first two lay donors, just after the awakening, as they set out on the road. The card needs none of that context — it is a blessing for anyone leaving the house.
