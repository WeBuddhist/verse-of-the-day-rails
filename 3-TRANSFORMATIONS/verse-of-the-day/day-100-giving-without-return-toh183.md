### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation: "Give without hoping for any return at all, and the fruit that ripens is unsurpassed, complete awakening."
- Findings: our modern Traditional rendering; the source is **Tibetan**, so this is a fresh rendering from the rail's meaning. 布施 is the `termbase.md` *dāna* cell. 覺悟 for `བྱང་ཆུབ` rather than 菩提 — the plainer, more immediately understood modern word, and it avoids the technical register the rail flags; 大乘 is nowhere near this line, per the ecumenical rule. 成熟的果實 keeps the ripening metaphor per flag (b). 任何 added to strengthen "any return at all," matching the source's emphasis.
- Confidence note: 無上圓滿 is faithful but formal; if a reviewer softens the English formula, this should soften with it.
- Net: clean for review.

### hi

- Findings: दान is the `termbase.md` *dāna* cell. फल पकता है keeps the ripening metaphor per flag (b) — Hindi carries this more naturally than English does. बोधि left as the established Sanskrit-derived term, which a Hindi Buddhist reader knows; अनुत्तर likewise. बदले में कुछ पाने की आशा बिना is a clean rendering of flag (c)'s sense.
- Confidence note: अनुत्तर पूर्ण बोधि is a Sanskritic register — appropriate for Hindi Buddhist usage, but confirm it is not stiffer than the rest of the line.
- Net: clean for review.
