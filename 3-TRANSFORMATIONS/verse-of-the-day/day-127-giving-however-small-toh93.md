# Day 127 — However Small (Toh 93)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/toh93-small-gift]] · own translation from the Degé Kangyur (Public Domain); 84000 English as reference only; Mahāyāna.
One sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the category is not sized. What makes it giving is what you gave it from.

> **⚑ Placed for the occasion.** 2026-10-26 is the October full moon: **Pavāraṇā, the end of the rains retreat, and the opening of Kathina season**, which `occasions.md` lists with **generosity** among its themes. The rail carries `occasions: [kathina]`. **Do not move this card off the date without deciding to give up the occasion fit** — though note `occasions.md` insists dates are lunar and resolved at runtime per tradition, so the tag is the durable part.

> **It removes a threshold rather than issuing an instruction.** Nothing is asked of the reader; something is lifted off them. That is why the card type is encouragement rather than teaching.

> **⚑ The sūtra's merit-arithmetic is deliberately excluded.** The next sentence opens a long series of cases (giving out of fear of the next world, giving to the disciplined and the undisciplined). Pulling any of it in would import both a length problem and a transactional frame. **Do not extend this quote.**

> **⚑ "true giving" was considered and dropped.** *"All of it counts as true giving"* lands the reassurance harder, but the Tibetan has no word for "true" — `ནི` is a topic-marker, not an adjective. The card ships without it. A reviewer may overrule, but it should be a decision rather than a drift.

> **⚑ This verse genuinely uses `སྦྱིན་པ` / *dāna*, unlike Day 122.** Day 122 (Toh 329) had to avoid `སྦྱིན་པ` because its source word was `གཏོང་བ` / *tyāga*. Here the Degé says `སྦྱིན་པ`, so the termbase `dāna` row applies directly in every language, `bo` included. The two cards sit on either side of that distinction rather than blurring it.

> **⚑ Note on bo:** the verbatim Degé sentence below is **the source**; the shipped `bo` is **modern, plain Tibetan** like every other language. **Do not "correct" it back.**

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**
