# Day 122 — What Giving and Kindness Do (Toh 329)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/toh329-giving-makes-friends]] · own translation from the Degé Kangyur (Public Domain); 84000 English as reference only.
Two padas answering two of a deity's four questions, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the people around you respond to how you treat them.

> **⚑ The one-idea flag was seen and overruled, not missed.** The pool held this entry as borderline — friends and enemies are arguably two claims, the axis Day 87 was rejected on. **Evan selected it anyway (2026-08-26)** on the reading that it is one idea in its two natural directions. A reviewer should know the flag exists rather than rediscover it.

> **Two of four padas, stopped at a clause boundary.** The deity asked four separate questions in one stanza and the Buddha answered one pada per question, so padas 3 and 4 are separate answers, not the rest of this thought. They also carry `མཐོ་རིས` (the higher realms), cosmology the card cannot gloss, and the full quatrain busts the budget in every language. The rail has the whole stanza and the reasoning.

> **⚑ The word here is not the *dāna* word.** 84000's note [n11] on this pada records that `གཏོང་བ` (*tyāga*, giving up / liberality) is deliberately distinct from `སྦྱིན་པ` (*dāna*, the perfection). The English, Chinese, Hindi, Nepali and Mongolian ship the ordinary giving sense; the `bo` line keeps `གཞན་ལ་སྟེར་བ` rather than substituting `སྦྱིན་པ`, which would import a technical frame the Degé does not have.

> **⚑ Note on bo:** the verbatim Degé line below is **the source**; the shipped `bo` is **modern, plain Tibetan** like every other language. **Do not "correct" it back.**

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**
