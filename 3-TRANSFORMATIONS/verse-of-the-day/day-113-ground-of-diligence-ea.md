# Day 113 — The Ground Everything Grows From (EĀ 卷18)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/ea-ground-of-diligence]] · own translation from the CBETA Chinese source (CC BY-NC-SA); no Patton translation exists for the Ekottarika, so SN 45.150 and AN 10.15 (Sujato, CC0) are cited as convergent references only.
Short sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: your good qualities are not separate projects, they share one root.

> **⚑ This card was approved as a simile and shipped as an observation.** The earth simile it came with (mountains, rivers, grasses and grain all grow from the earth) renders at **200 characters** whole, which fails the ~125 gate, and it cannot be shortened without distilling. The card therefore ships the passage's **second sentence alone**. Full reasoning, and the two alternates that could replace this card, in the rail.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**

## Source (Chinese, EĀ 卷18 — verbatim)

Full passage in the block:
> 猶如山河、石壁、百草、五穀，皆依於地而得長大，然復此地最尊、最上。此亦如是，諸善道品之法，住不放逸之地，使諸善法而得長大。 (`zh-ekottarika-agama.md#^pT02p0635b1202`)

The shipped sentence, verbatim from it:
> 諸善道品之法，住不放逸之地，使諸善法而得長大。

- Back-translation: n/a (anchor rendering).
- Findings: all three elements of the shipped sentence present — the good qualities, the ground of diligence, the growing ✓. **`不放逸` ships as "diligence"** per the rail flag, matching Sujato at `^an10-15-1-2` and Day 77, not "heedfulness" (Day 66, already flagged for accessibility). **`地` kept as "ground"** per the rail flag, since it is the last trace of the excluded simile. **`諸善道品之法` ships as "every good quality of the path"**, not "the factors of the path," which would need a gloss. The Pali parallels were **not** allowed to overwrite the Chinese: SN 45.150 grounds the simile on ethics rather than diligence, and that reading is deliberately not imported ✓. 101 chars. No em dash ✓. No "Buddha" in the card ✓.
- Net: clean for review, with the rail's "arrives already applied" caveat carried forward — a reviewer may judge that the reader still has one small step to take.

### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation (against the verbatim source, per rule 8): "All the good qualities on the path of practice stand on this ground of non-negligence, and it is precisely from this ground that they grow." Maps onto 諸善道品之法，住不放逸之地，使諸善法而得長大 with no drift; the doubled 諸善…諸善法 of the source is not reproduced twice in modern Chinese, which would read redundantly, and is carried once with `也正是` supplying the resumption.
- Findings: **our modern rendering, not the CBETA text.** Substitutions, each checked: `諸善道品之法` → `修道上一切善的品質`; `住…之地` → `都立在…這片土地上` (which keeps the earth word 土地 rather than abstracting to 基礎); `使…而得長大` → `也正是從這片土地生長起來的`. **不放逸 is kept unchanged** — it is the termbase term and remains legible in modern Chinese, so modernising it would lose the doctrinal anchor for no gain. 36 chars. Taiwan/HK/SG register ✓, Traditional ✓, genuinely modern rather than half-classical ✓.
- Net: clean for review.

### hi
- Back-translation: "All the wholesome qualities of the path rest on the ground of अप्रमाद, and from that same ground they grow."
- Findings: कुशल is the termbase row for *kusala* ✓; अप्रमाद is the termbase row for *appamāda* ✓ and is a live Hindi Buddhist term, not a coinage. भूमि keeps `地`/"ground" concrete per the rail flag ✓. टिके हैं ("rest / are propped") carries `住` better than आधारित हैं, which would abstract it.
- Net: clean for review.

### bo ⚑

(iv) `སྐྱེ་ཞིང་འཕེལ` renders `長大` as arise-and-increase; confirm the growth sense survives and is not merely arising. (v) Register: modern and plain, but confirm it does not land in a half-classical middle register. (vi) The card is Chinese-source, so this line has no Kangyur wording behind it.
