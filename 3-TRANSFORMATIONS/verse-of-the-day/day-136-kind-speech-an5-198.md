# Day 136 — Kind Speech (AN 5.198)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/an-5-198]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0).
One teaching, **quoted in full** (the five factors + their frame). Meaning: what makes speech good — right timing, truth, gentleness, usefulness, and a kind heart behind it.

> **A unified list, not mixed ideas.** Five qualities of one thing (well-spoken speech), so it's fine under the list ruling. Keep the parallel five in every rendering.

> **⚑ The fifth factor is mettā — ship it per termbase** (慈 / བྱམས་ / मैत्री), rendered "kind/loving," not flattened to generic niceness. It is what turns correct speech into warm speech.

> **⚑ This is the warm, positive counterpart to a prohibition, not an ethics rule.** It teaches how to speak well; it does not police wrongdoing. (Recorded because a recent harsh-speech card was set aside as "too much ethics.")

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line.**

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an5-198-1-1]]

> Kālena ca bhāsitā hoti, saccā ca bhāsitā hoti, saṇhā ca bhāsitā hoti, atthasaṁhitā ca bhāsitā hoti, mettacittena ca bhāsitā hoti. (`^an5-198-1-3`)

## Renderings (one short line each)
- **en** — Speech that is timely, true, gentle, helpful, and kind: this is speech well spoken.
- **zh** — 說話合乎時機、真實、溫和、有益、慈愛，這就是善說之語。
