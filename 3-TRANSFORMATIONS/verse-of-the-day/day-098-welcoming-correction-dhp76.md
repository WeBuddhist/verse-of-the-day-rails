# Day 98 — Welcoming Correction (Dhp 76)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/dhp-76]] · grounded directly in Bhikkhu Sujato's English (CC0); Pali source also CC0.
Short verse, **quoted in full** (opening portion, stopped at a clause boundary). Meaning: the person who tells you what you got wrong is handing you something valuable.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi (`lbj`) discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. The rule docs still say seven (the decision was "stop going forward only"); **do not add an `lbj` line back to this card on the strength of them.**

## Source (Pali, Dhammapada)

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-dhammapada.md#^76]]

> *Nidhīnaṁva pavattāraṁ, yaṁ passe vajjadassinaṁ; Niggayhavādiṁ medhāviṁ, tādisaṁ paṇḍitaṁ bhaje.* (`pi-dhammapada.md#^76`)

## Renderings (one short line each)

> **Note on order:** every language puts the plain statement **before** the simile, where the Pali leads with the simile. Deliberate — see the rail's flag (a). A literal order reads as a riddle in these languages, and riddle-shaped cards have been rejected.

- **en** — Keep company with one wise enough to point out your faults, as you would keep a guide to hidden treasure.
- **zh** — 要親近那位明智到能指出你過失的人，就像親近為你指出藏寶之處的人一樣。
- **bo** — ⚑ ཁྱོད་ཀྱི་སྐྱོན་གསལ་པོར་སྟོན་ཐུབ་པའི་མཁས་པ་དེ་དང་འགྲོགས་ཤིག གཏེར་གནས་སྟོན་མཁན་དང་འགྲོགས་པ་བཞིན།
- **hi** — जो इतना समझदार हो कि तुम्हारी गलतियाँ बता सके, उसका साथ रखो, जैसे छिपे खजाने का पता बताने वाले का।
- **ne** — जो यति बुद्धिमान् छ कि तिम्रो गल्ती देखाइदिन सक्छ, त्यसको साथ राख, जसरी लुकेको धनको ठाउँ बताउनेको राख्छौ।
- **mn** — ⚑ Чиний бурууг зааж чадах ухаантай хүнтэй нөхөрлө, далд эрдэнэсийн газрыг заасан хүнтэй нөхөрлөх шиг.

### en
- Back-translation: n/a (source language of the rail's Disambiguated Meaning).
- Findings: **order reversed from the source** per rail flag (a) — plain statement first, simile second; flagged as a real departure from the Pali's rhetoric, taken because a simile-first English line reads as a riddle. **"Point out your faults"** for `vajjadassin` per flag (b), avoiding "critic" (hostile, modern) and "fault-finder" (implies a habit, the opposite of the meaning). **`niggayhavādin` and `medhāvin` compressed** into "one wise enough to point out your faults" per flag (c) — a wording compression, both senses present. **"Hidden treasure"** for `nidhi` per flag (d); the concealment is what makes the information valuable, so "riches" or "a gift" would break the image. **"Keep company with"** for `bhaje` per flag (e), an ongoing association rather than a one-off listen. **The closing couplet is absent** — a restatement of the benefit, dropped for length, per the rail's flag ✓. 105 chars. No em dash ✓.
- Net: clean for review. Note this is the batch's most bracing card — the rail asks a reviewer to confirm it reads as generous rather than as a telling-off.
