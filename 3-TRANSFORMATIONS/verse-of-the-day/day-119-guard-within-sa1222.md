# Day 119 — Where the Guard Belongs (SĀ 1222)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/sa-1222-guard-within]] · own translation from the CBETA Chinese source (CC BY-NC-SA), grounded on the Pali parallel SN 3.5 (Sujato, CC0). No Patton translation exists for SĀ 1222.
Short sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: safety is not something you build around your life, it is how you conduct it.

> **⚑ Speaker note.** In both recensions King Pasenadi says this first and the Buddha then affirms and repeats it (`佛告大王：「如是，大王！如是，大王！…」` / *"That's so true, great king!… And he repeated the king's statement"*). The shipped sentence is inside the Buddha's own speech, so the gate is met, but the insight originated with the king. A reviewer should know rather than discover it.

## Source (Chinese, SĀ 1222 — verbatim)

Passage in the block:
> 佛告大王：「…若復有行身善行、行口善行、行意善行者，當知斯等則為自護。彼雖不以象、馬、車、步四軍自防，而實自護。所以者何？護其內者，名善自護，非謂防外。」 (`zh-samyukta-agama.md#^pT02p0336b0610`)

> 護其內者，名善自護，非謂防外。

### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation (against the verbatim source, per rule 8): "Guarding what is inside is what really protects yourself; it is not defending what is outside." Maps onto 護其內者，名善自護，非謂防外 clause for clause, no drift.
- Findings: **our modern rendering, not the CBETA text.** Substitutions, each checked: `護其內者` → `守護內在的`; `名善自護` → `才是真正保護自己`; `非謂防外` → `不是防守外面的`. The classical `者…名…非謂…` naming construction is replaced by the modern `才是…不是…` contrast, which is what carries a reframe in modern Chinese ✓. ⚑ **守護/保護 is used here where Day 117 uses 照顧 for the same verb family** — deliberate: this card is about protection, Day 117 about looking after. A reviewer should confirm the distinction is right rather than harmonise them silently. 22 chars, the shortest zh line in recent batches. Taiwan/HK/SG register ✓, Traditional ✓.
- Net: clean for review.

### hi
