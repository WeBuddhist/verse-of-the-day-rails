# Day 78 — Sympathetic Joy (EĀ 增壹阿含經)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/ea-mudita]] · own translation from CBETA Chinese source (CC BY-NC-SA), grounded on the Pali parallel MN 62 (Sujato, CC0).
Short sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: gladness at another's good fortune is a practice, and it is what clears envy out.

## Source (Chinese, EĀ — verbatim)

> 當行喜心，已行喜心，所有嫉心皆當除盡。 (`zh-ekottarika-agama.md#^pT02p0581c1216`)

## QA — pre-review (against 2-RAILS/Verses/ea-mudita.md)

### en
- Back-translation: "Cultivate sympathetic joy. Once sympathetic joy is cultivated, all envy is entirely cleared away."
- Findings: all three beats of the rail present — instruction, the "once practised" conditional, and the emphatic result. "Sympathetic joy" is the termbase rendering of *muditā*, not "happiness" (rail flag b). "All … entirely cleared away" holds 皆 and 除盡's emphatic force (flag c). Ships the Chinese source's **envy** (嫉心), not the Pali parallel's *arati* / "discontent" — the flagged divergence, deliberately not flattened. ~99 chars. No em dash ✓.
- Net: clean for review.

### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation: "You should cultivate sympathetic joy; once sympathetic joy has been cultivated, all envy will be thoroughly removed."
- Findings: **our own modern Traditional rendering**, not the verbatim source (per the note above). What was modernised: classical 當 as an imperative particle becomes the plain modern 要; the terse 已行 becomes 修習起來之後 ("after it has been cultivated"), which makes the conditional explicit where classical Chinese leaves it to juxtaposition; 所有…皆 becomes the modern 一切…都; and 除盡 becomes 徹底去除, keeping the "removed to exhaustion" force per rail flag (c). 喜心 rendered **隨喜心** rather than the bare 喜心 — the termbase gives 喜 / 隨喜 for *muditā*, and 隨喜 is what carries "joy at another's good" in modern Chinese, whereas a modern reader may take 喜心 as general cheerfulness (rail flag b). 修習 for 行 as "cultivate / practise" per flag (a). Taiwan/HK/SG register, not mainland. Ships the source's **envy** (嫉妒), not the Pali parallel's *arati*.
- Net: clean for review.

### hi
- Back-translation: "Practise muditā. Once muditā has been practised, all envy fades away completely."
- Findings: मुदिता is the termbase term, kept as the loanword rather than paraphrased to खुशी ("happiness"), which would lose the "joy at another's good" sense (rail flag b). ईर्ष्या = envy, matching 嫉 not *arati*. अभ्यास carries 行 as "practise" (flag a).
- Net: clean for review.
