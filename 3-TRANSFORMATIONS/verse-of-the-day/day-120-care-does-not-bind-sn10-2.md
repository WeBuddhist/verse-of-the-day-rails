# Day 120 — Caring Does Not Tie You Down (SN 10.2)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/sn10-2-care-does-not-bind]] · grounded in Sujato (CC0), directly aligned at the same four block anchors.
Short verse, **quoted in full**. Meaning: caring about people does not cost you your freedom, if the heart it comes from is clear.

> **⚑ The Pali is impersonal; the second person is Sujato's.** The stanza has a conditional with a third-person verb and no pronoun of any person. Sujato supplies "you" and "your" — the standing Nikāya finding, same as Days 103 and 117. Weaker case than Day 114, where the second person was ours rather than the translator's.

> **Why two earlier scans missed it.** Not a pronoun problem but a **speaker-attribution** one: SN 10 is the yakkha collection, flagged as almost entirely non-buddhavacana, so the whole block gets discarded wholesale — **but the Buddha's replies inside it are his own words.** The same is true of SN 1 and SN 11.

## QA — pre-review (against 2-RAILS/Verses/sn10-2-care-does-not-bind.md)

### en
- Back-translation: n/a (anchor rendering).
- Findings: all four padas present — the clear mind, the guiding of another, the not-being-bound, and the naming of what does not bind ✓. **`anusāsati` ships as "guide,"** not Sujato's "instruct" (schoolmasterly in modern English) or "advise" (transactional). **`pasanna` ships as "a clear and confident heart"** rather than "mind," since "a clear mind" suggests concentration in English and every other language here uses the heart-mind word. **`anukampā anuddayā` ships as "kindness and care" — deliberately NOT "compassion,"** which the termbase reserves for *karuṇā*, a different word this verse does not use; Sujato's "empathy" reads clinically. ⚑ **`anukampā` has no termbase row and one should be added if this family recurs** — noted rather than coined. **`saṁyutta` ships as "tie you down"**, a rope word rendered as a felt cost rather than the abstract "create attachments." The spirit's challenge and the vocative stanza are excluded ✓. 100 chars. No em dash ✓. No "Buddha" in the card ✓.
- Net: clean for review, with the impersonal-Pali disclosure and the Day 117 adjacency carried forward.

### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation: "If you guide others with a clear and settled mind, your goodwill and care will not tie you up."
- Findings: source is Pali, so no classical-source question. 清明安定 renders `pasanna` as clear-and-settled, avoiding 清淨 which would read as ritual purity ✓. 善意與關懷 for `anukampā anuddayā` — deliberately **not** 慈悲, which is the termbase's *karuṇā* rendering and would claim a row this verse does not use ✓. 綁住 keeps `saṁyutta` as a rope rather than the abstract 執著 ✓. 引導 for `anusāsati` avoids 教導, which would make it a lecture ✓. 28 chars. Taiwan/HK/SG register ✓, Traditional ✓.
- Net: clean for review.

### hi
- Back-translation: "If you show someone the way with a clean and steady mind, then your good-heartedness and caring will not bind you."
- Findings: राह दिखाते हो for `anusāsati` keeps guiding rather than instructing ✓. भलमनसाहत और परवाह for `anukampā anuddayā` — deliberately **not** करुणा, the termbase's *karuṇā* row ✓; both words are everyday Hindi, matching the card's plain register. बाँधेगी नहीं keeps the binding image ✓. ⚑ साफ़ for `pasanna` is plain but may read as "clean/tidy"; निर्मल would be closer to the clarity sense but more Sanskritic. Flagged as a live choice.
- Net: clean for review, with the साफ़ / निर्मल choice flagged.

- Net: needs native review.

### bo ⚑
- Back-translation (approx): "If you guide another with a clear and steady mind, your loving heart and caring heart cannot bind you."
- Confidence flags: (i) ⚑ **`བྱམས་སེམས` is the termbase's *mettā* term (`བྱམས་པ`) and this verse is not about *mettā*.** It is used because Tibetan has no ready plain word for `anukampā`, but a reviewer should either confirm it or supply one (`སྙིང་བརྩེ`? `བརྩེ་སེམས`?) — this is the weakest decision on the line and it silently borrows a row the verse does not use. (ii) `སེམས་དང་བ` for `pasanna` uses `དང་བ`, which is the standard Tibetan for the settled/confident mind and is a good match — confirm. (iii) `བཅིང་མི་སྲིད` ("cannot bind") is stronger than the source's plain negative — confirm. (iv) `ལམ་སྟོན` for `anusāsati` — confirm it reads as guiding rather than road-directions. (v) Register: modern and plain; confirm no half-classical drift. (vi) The card is Pali-source, so this line has no Kangyur wording behind it.
- Net: escalate to native Tibetan dharma reviewer.
