# Day 108 — Goodness Is Happiness (AN 7.62)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/an7-62-dont-fear-good]] · grounded directly in Bhikkhu Sujato's English (CC0); Pali source also CC0.
Short prose pair, **quoted in full**. Meaning: doing good isn't a cost you pay for happiness — it's the same thing.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03; the rule docs still say seven. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**

## Source (Pali, Aṅguttara Nikāya)

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an7-62-1-1]]
![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an7-62-1-2]]

> *Mā, bhikkhave, puññānaṁ bhāyittha. Sukhassetaṁ, bhikkhave, adhivacanaṁ yadidaṁ puññāni.* (`pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an7-62-1-1`–`^an7-62-1-2`)

The sutta then continues into the Buddha's own past lives as Brahmā and Sakka. **None of that is in the quote** — see the rail's containment flag.

## QA — pre-review (against 2-RAILS/Verses/an7-62-dont-fear-good.md)

### en
- Findings: **`adhivacana` ships as "just another word for"** per rail flag (a) — the identity claim. "Leads to," "brings" and "results in" were all rejected because they reinstate exactly the trade-off the sutta denies; this is the card's whole point and the easiest thing to lose. **`puñña` ships as "doing good," not "merit"** — a deliberate `termbase.md` divergence per the rail's ⚑ flag: "do not be afraid of merit" is not English and fails the collocation screen, and Sujato reaches the same conclusion. **"Do not be afraid"** keeps the prohibitive a reassurance rather than a command, per flag (b). Vocative dropped. **The Brahmā/Sakka past-life material is absent** ✓. 72 chars. No em dash ✓.
- Net: clean for review.

### zh (Traditional)
- Back-translation: "Do not be afraid of doing good. 'Good' was always just another name for happiness."

Taiwan/HK/SG register.
