# Day 115 — Your Shelter and Your Support (DĀ 2)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/da-2-shelter]] · own translation from the CBETA Chinese source (CC BY-NC-SA), grounded on the Pali parallel DN 16 (Sujato, CC0).
Short sentence, **quoted in full**. Meaning: the shelter is not a person you might lose, it is already in your hands.

> **⚑ Two things a reviewer should weigh.** (1) The Pali parallel says the teaching will be your **teacher** (`satthā`); the Chinese says it is your **shelter** (`護`). The card ships the Chinese and must not be "corrected" to the Pali. (2) `成佛來` literally reads "since I became a Buddha" — the card ships "since my awakening" and the `zh` line ships `成道以來`, to keep the word *Buddha* off the card per Evan's 2026-08-03 screen. The card is still in the Buddha's first person, and whether the screen reaches that far is an open item in `card-types.md`.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi discontinued by Evan, 2026-08-03. **Do not add an `lbj` line back.**
