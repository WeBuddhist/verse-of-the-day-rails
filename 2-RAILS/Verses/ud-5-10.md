## Source Text

**⚑ The card ships the verse's FIRST sentence only (padas 1–4), and this is a
deliberate, rule-compliant cut — read this before touching it.** Ud 5.10's udāna
is six padas / two sentences. The first sentence (`^ud5-10-4-1` … `^ud5-10-4-4`)
is grammatically complete on its own: *"steady in body and mind, whether
standing, sitting or lying down, a mendicant keeping to this mindfulness would
gain progressive distinction."* The second sentence (`^ud5-10-4-5` …
`^ud5-10-4-6`) adds *"and having gained it, would go beyond the sight of the
King of Death."* The vault's rule permits **"a complete verse OR one
self-contained sentence, quoted in full"** — the card ships the first sentence,
quoted whole, which (a) keeps a real grammatical unit and (b) keeps the
`maccurāja` / death image off a daily card. **Do not extend the quote to the
death line, and do not treat this as a stitched fragment — it is one whole
sentence.**

## Disambiguated Meaning

Steadiness of body and of mind (`ṭhitena kāyena ṭhitena cetasā`) carried through
every posture — standing, sitting, lying down (`tiṭṭhaṁ nisinno uda vā sayāno`).
A practitioner who keeps to this presence (`etaṁ satiṁ ... adhiṭṭhahāno`) gains a
distinction that grows earlier-and-later, i.e. steadily over time (`labhetha
pubbāpariyaṁ visesaṁ`)
(1-SOURCES/Translations/en-udana-sujato.md#^ud5-10-4-4). The teaching is that
presence is not confined to formal sitting — it is available in any posture,
across the ordinary day, and it compounds.

**⚑ Rendering choice flagged — `etaṁ satiṁ` compressed to "stay present."**
`sati` here is mindfulness/presence, and `etaṁ` ("this") points to the
posture-spanning steadiness just named. "Stay present in this" collapses "keep
to this mindfulness" into plain language; a reviewer should confirm it does not
lose the *keeping-to / maintaining* force of `adhiṭṭhahāno` (it is not a
one-off, but a sustained holding). Flagged, not fixed.

**⚑ `pubbāpariyaṁ visesaṁ` ("progressive / earlier-and-later distinction")
ships as "you only grow."** The Pali says the distinction accrues over time; the
card renders the growth without the technical "distinction." A reviewer may want
something closer to "you keep growing / grow steadily." Flagged.

## Theme & Selection Notes

**Theme:** `present-moment`. Distinct from Day 102 (`present-moment`, Toh 313,
the Bhaddekaratta "auspicious night" of not chasing past or future): that one is
*don't live in past/future*; this one is *presence is available in every
posture, all day*. Different angle, and 31 days apart. Fresh image (standing /
sitting / lying).

**Card type: `teaching`** — it tells the reader what to do (stay present in any
posture) and states the result. Keeps the type adjacency legal against Day 133
(`simile`, Ud 4.4, same batch) and Day 132 (`observation`).

**One-second test ✓.** "Steady in body and mind — standing, sitting, or lying
down — stay present, and you grow." Read at speed it lands: be present whatever
you are doing, and it builds.

**Not bleak ✓.** Wholly constructive; the death image that closes the full verse
is exactly what the first-sentence cut removes, so the card ends on growth.
