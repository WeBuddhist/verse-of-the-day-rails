## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Chinese source (`zh-ekottarika-agama.md#^pT02p0564a0501`):

1. **`不觸嬈一切眾生`** — "do not **disturb / provoke** (`觸嬈` — to stir up, meddle with, bother) any living being (`一切眾生`)."
2. **`不害一切眾生`** — "do not **harm** (`害` — injure, do damage to) any living being."
3. **`不惱一切眾生`** — "do not **vex / distress** (`惱` — cause annoyance or mental upset to) any living being."

The three verbs are a graded set, and the grading is the point: not merely *do not injure* (the obvious one) but also *do not bother* and *do not irritate*. It sets the bar at leaving things undisturbed rather than merely unbroken, and it applies the rule to `一切眾生` — everything alive, with no exception carved out.

- **⚑ Both the frame and the label are deliberately dropped, and the label matters most.** The source reads `云何無放逸行？所謂 [quote] 是謂無放逸行` — "what is non-negligence? It is [quote]. This is called non-negligence." **Shipping `是謂無放逸行` would make the card about a technical label** (`無放逸` / *appamāda*) rather than about the reader's own conduct, and the label would need a glossary. The quote therefore stops before it. This is the same move as Day 93's negative-mirror drop and Day 99's caste-argument drop: keep the substance, leave the scaffolding.
- **⚑ Register risk, stated plainly: this is an instruction, not a consolation.** It asks something of the reader, where most recent accepted cards give something. It survives the warmth screen because it is **gentle in scale** — the ask is to leave things alone, not to achieve anything — and because it condemns nobody. But it is the most instructional card in this batch, and a reviewer should confirm it reads as a quiet standard rather than a rule being imposed.
- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) **The three verbs must stay distinct** — "trouble / harm / vex" in English. Collapsing them ("do no harm to any living being") would destroy the grading that is the whole point, and would also make the card a platitude. (b) `一切眾生` ships as **"living being"** rather than "sentient being" (glossary-flavoured) or "all beings" (which in a Buddhist register pulls toward the cosmological). The plainness is deliberate. (c) The **triple repetition of "no living being" is kept** in every language rather than compressed to a single object — the incantatory repetition is the source's form and carries the sense of no-exceptions. (d) The imperative is negative in all three clauses, matching the source's `不`; a positive recast ("treat every living thing gently") was considered and rejected as a paraphrase rather than a translation.
