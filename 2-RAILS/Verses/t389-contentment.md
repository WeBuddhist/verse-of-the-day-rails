## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Chinese source (`zh-bequeathed-teachings.md#^pT12p1111c0501`):

The sentence is a **chiasmus** on 知足 (*santuṭṭhi*, contentment — termbase: contentment · 知足 · ཆོག་ཤེས · संतोष · सन्तोष · ханамж ⚑):

1. **`不知足者雖富而貧`** — "one who does not know enough (`不知足`), though rich (`雖富`), is poor (`而貧`)."
2. **`知足之人雖貧而富`** — "the person who knows enough, though poor, is rich."

`雖…而…` is the concessive "although X, yet Y," used twice in mirror image, so the sentence's whole force is the **reversal of the ordinary measure**: wealth and poverty are relocated from what you hold to whether you have stopped needing more. Nothing is added about how to become content, and nothing is said against having things — the claim is only about where rich and poor actually sit.

- **⚑ Rendering choice: 不知足 must not become "greedy."** 不知足 is literally *not-knowing-enough*, a state of never arriving, not a moral accusation. "Never satisfied" carries it; "greedy," "grasping" or "covetous" all import a condemnation the sentence does not make, and would flip the card from a reframe (which the reader receives with relief) into a rebuke (which fails the warmth screen). Same caution in every language.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: 富 / 貧 stay concrete.** "Rich" and "poor," not "abundant" / "impoverished" or "fulfilled" / "lacking." Abstracting them dissolves the reversal, which only works because the two words mean money in the reader's ordinary use.
- **⚑ Do not import the host passage.** The block continues `不知足者常為五欲所牽，為知足者之所憐愍` ("the discontented are constantly dragged by the five sense-desires, and are pitied by the content"). That is a **separate sentence**, it is admonishing in tone, and 五欲 needs a gloss. **Excluded** — the card is the chiasmus alone. The block also opens with `知足之人，雖臥地上猶為安樂；不知足者，雖處天堂亦不稱意` (lying on bare ground vs. being in heaven), which is the same teaching in a weaker form and imports a heaven-frame; also excluded. Do not later "restore" either as if the card had been truncated.
- **Concessive structure must survive.** Each language needs a real "although X, still Y" construction. A rendering that flattens to "the discontented are poor and the content are rich" loses the concession and with it the surprise.
