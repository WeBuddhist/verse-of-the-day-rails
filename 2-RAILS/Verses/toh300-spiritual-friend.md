## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Tibetan source (`bo-toh300.md#^p610`) and the 84000 reference (`en-toh300-84000.md`). Three near-synonyms, then one verb:

- **`དགེ་བའི་བཤེས་གཉེན`** — the *kalyāṇa-mitta*, the **spiritual friend** (termbase: good friend, spiritual friend · 善知識 · དགེ་བའི་བཤེས་གཉེན · कल्याणमित्र). Note this is emphatically **not** *sahāya*, the plain companion of Day 1 (Dhp 328) — the termbase keeps these apart on purpose and warns against rendering one with the other's word.
- **`དགེ་བའི་གྲོགས་པོ`** — a **good companion**, the everyday warm word.
- **`དགེ་བའི་སྟེན`** — a **good support / one to rely on** (`སྟེན` = to lean on, depend on).
- **`དང་གནས་པར་བྱའི`** — "one should **live/dwell with**" them. Not "seek out" or "admire": the instruction is about who you keep company with.

The sūtra's frame gives the claim its weight: the Buddha says this **as he is about to pass into parinirvāṇa** (`en-toh300-84000.md`, opening), and when Ānanda proposes that good spiritual friendship is *half* the holy life, the Buddha corrects him — it is the **whole** of it (same file). That correction is not shipped (it needs Ānanda's line to make sense, an out-of-context fail under §2), but it is why this instruction is not a minor courtesy.

- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) The three terms are near-synonyms and each language should find **three distinct everyday words**, not one word repeated — collapsing them would flatten a deliberate triple. (b) `དགེ་བ` here means "good / wholesome" in the ordinary sense, not the technical *kusala* of a karma verse; a plain adjective is right. (c) `གནས་པ` is "dwell/live with," and the companionable sense should survive; "associate with" reads clinical. (d) The vocative `དགེ་སློང་དག` ("monks") is dropped so the instruction reads universally — the same treatment as `toh231-merit-with-respect` and `toh59-root-of-illwill`.

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **spiritual-friendship** (*kalyāṇa-mittatā*) — **new to the calendar and a documented product gap.** `discovery-by-feeling.md`'s Door B table lists "spiritual friendship / sangha (*kalyāṇa-mittatā*) — **the Connect pillar**; 'the whole of the holy life'" with SN 45.2 marked ⬜ *acquire*. Distinct from Day 1's `friendship` (Dhp 328, *sahāya*, a good travelling companion) — different Pali term, different termbase row. Gate checks: buddhavacana (the Buddha addressing the monks directly); one complete instruction, quoted whole; intelligible with no backstory; concrete and warm, and it tells the reader what to *do*; not a proclamation of attainment. In scope (mdo). Length: bo 74 chars, en 68 — comfortably inside budget, the shortest card in this batch. License: Degé PD source; own rendering; 84000 reference only.
