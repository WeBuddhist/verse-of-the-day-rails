## Disambiguated Meaning
Four lines, grounded in the Tibetan source (`bo-toh313.md#^p326`), with 84000's English consulted as a meaning reference only:

1. **`འདས་ལ་རྗེས་སུ་ཕྲད་མི་བྱེད`** — "do not **go to meet** (`རྗེས་སུ་ཕྲད`) the past." See the ⚑ textual note: the Degé verb is *meet / encounter*, not *follow*.
2. **`མ་འོངས་པ་ལ་རེ་བ་མེད`** — "have no **hope / expectation** (`རེ་བ`) toward the future."
3. **`འདས་པ་གང་ཡིན་དེ་འགགས་ཏེ`** — "whatever is past **has stopped** (`འགགས`)."
4. **`མ་འོངས་དེ་ནི་མ་ཕྱིན་པའོ`** — "and the future **has not arrived** (`མ་ཕྱིན་པ`)."

The stanza's move is to give a reason immediately after the instruction. Lines 1–2 tell the reader what not to do; lines 3–4 say why, and the why is not a moral argument but a plain observation about availability: neither of those two places exists to be occupied. Nothing is condemned; the reader is simply shown that two of the three directions attention runs in are empty.

- **⚑ TEXTUAL TRAP — caught, and the second time 84000 has done this.** 84000's note [n6] states that **all Kangyur recensions read `རྗེས་སུ་ཕྲད`** ("meet / encounter") and that **84000 emended it to `རྗེས་སུ་འབྲང`** ("follow") on the strength of the Sanskrit witness SI 2044 (`anvāgamayed`). **Our source is the Degé, so we ground on `ཕྲད`.** The two readings converge in sense — "do not go back to the past" carries both — so the card is safe either way, but the rail must record it. (84000's note [n7] confirms the Degé reads `རེ་བ` in line 2, so no issue there.) **This is the same category of problem as the Toh 329 emendation flagged on Day 97's rail: check 84000's footnotes before trusting its English.**
- **⚑ Containment flag — what surrounds this stanza must NOT be carried over.** The same block continues into a stanza on **dying tomorrow** (`སང་ཙམ་ཤི་ཡང་སུ་ཤེས་ཀྱིས`, "who knows whether you will die tomorrow") and Death's great army. That is exactly the sober material the calendar has repeatedly rejected, and it is not in this quote. Do not extend the card to include it.
- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) `རྗེས་སུ་ཕྲད་མི་བྱེད` ships as **"do not go back to the past"** — a plain reading of "do not go to meet it." Avoid "do not dwell on," which is therapy vocabulary, and "do not pursue," which is 84000's emended reading, not ours. (b) `རེ་བ` is **hope / expectation**, and the rendering keeps "hope" — this is worth watching, since English "do not hope" can sound bleak; "do not hope for the future" is retained because the next two lines immediately supply the non-bleak reason. A reviewer should confirm it lands as freeing rather than as despairing. (c) `འགགས` is **has stopped / has ceased**; "has ended" is used on the card as the plainer English. (d) The four lines ship as **two sentences** rather than four, because English cannot carry four short verse lines as a card without reading like a list.
