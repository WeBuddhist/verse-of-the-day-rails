## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Chinese source (`zh-ekottarika-agama.md#^pT02p0600a0602`) and the Pali parallel (`pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an2-32-2-5`; Sujato at `en-anguttara-nikaya-sujato.md#^an2-32-2-5`):

1. **`若有眾生知反復者，此人可敬`** — "if there is anyone who **knows how to requite** (`知反復`, knows to return what was done for them), **that person is worthy of respect** (`可敬`)." The Pali equivalent quality is `kataññū-katavedī` — "grateful and thankful," or more literally *knowing what was done* and *making it known*; Sujato: "The true person is grateful and thankful." Both texts treat this as a **mark of character**, not a duty discharged.
2. **`小恩尚不忘，何況大恩`** — "even a **small** kindness (`小恩`) they still do not forget (`尚不忘`) — **how much more** (`何況`) a great one (`大恩`)." A classical Chinese *a fortiori* construction: the small case is stated, and the large follows without needing to be argued.

The teaching's move is to relocate gratitude. It is not measured by the size of what you received — that would make gratitude a matter of accounting. It is shown precisely in the small case, where nothing obliges you, and that is why it reveals what kind of person you are.

- **⚑ The negative mirror is deliberately excluded, and this is the card's central selection decision.** The source continues, at length, into the ungrateful person (`諸有眾生不知反復者…`, "those who do not requite do not even recall a great kindness"), and the Buddha's fuller point contrasts the two. **The card ships only the positive half.** This follows the Day 80 precedent (Toh 300, where the negative mirror "do not live with a bad friend" was likewise left off on tone grounds), and it is what makes this a card that praises rather than one that warns. Quoting the opening portion and stopping is permitted (`selection-criteria.md` §2). **Do not restore the negative half** — with it, this becomes the register that got Day 89 rejected.
- **⚑ Divergence from the Pali parallel — flagged, not flattened.** AN 2.32 anchors claim 1 (gratitude as the mark of a true person) closely and reliably. **It does not contain claim 2.** The small-kindness / great-kindness *a fortiori* is the **Chinese text's own addition**, with no Pali counterpart at this anchor. So the parallel grounds half the quote; the other half rests on the Chinese source alone. This is an honest limit of the grounding and should not be papered over by implying AN 2.32 says more than it does.
- **⚑ Dedupe note that matters: AN 2.32 already ran as Day 41** (2026-08-03, `an2-32-gratitude`, theme `gratitude`). This card is the **Chinese-canon witness of the same teaching**, fifty-two days later, with a claim the Pali lacks. The shipped text is entirely different and the angle is different (Day 41: grateful people are hard to find; this card: the small kindness is where gratitude shows). Acceptable under the theme-recurrence rule, but a curator should know the two cards are relatives.
- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) `知反復` is **"repays kindness" / "knows how to return kindness,"** not merely "is grateful" — the Chinese term is about *reciprocating*, which is stronger than a feeling. But avoid "repay a debt," which makes it transactional and undoes the point. (b) `可敬` is **"worthy of respect,"** not "admirable" or "praiseworthy" — the word is about being *rightly honoured* by others. (c) The `何況` construction should stay an **a fortiori** ("how much more"), **not** be turned into "let alone" or "still less," which inverts the logic — the source is saying the great kindness is *certainly* remembered, not that it is forgotten. This is the easiest error to make on this card. (d) `恩` is **kindness received / a favour done for you**, not "grace" and not "gift."

**Selected specifically against the screen that killed three picks in this batch's proposal** (see `rejected.md`, "Rejected on the SELF-APPLYING screen"). This quote is **self-applying and complete on its face**: it names no person, no place, no scene and no event; it addresses anyone (`若有眾生`, "if there is anyone"); and a reader knows on one read what it is telling them.
