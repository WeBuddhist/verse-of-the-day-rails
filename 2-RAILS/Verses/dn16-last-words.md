## Disambiguated Meaning
Two clauses, grounded in the source (`pi-digha-nikaya.md#^dn16-6-7-3`) and Sujato (`en-digha-nikaya-sujato.md#^dn16-6-7-3`):

1. **`vayadhammā saṅkhārā`** — *saṅkhārā* (conditioned, put-together things) are *vaya-dhamma*, "of a nature to decay / bound to fall away." Sujato renders it "Conditions fall apart" (`en-digha-nikaya-sujato.md#^dn16-6-7-3`). A statement of fact, not a lament.
2. **`appamādena sampādetha`** — the imperative: *sampādetha*, "accomplish it / bring it to completion / strive," done **`appamādena`**, "with diligence / heedfulness" (*appamāda*; termbase: diligence, heedfulness · 不放逸 · བག་ཡོད · अप्रमाद · अप्रमाद · сэрэмж ⚑). Sujato: "Persist with diligence."

The force of the sentence is the join between the two: because everything assembled comes apart, the response is not despair but diligence. Context confirmed by the very next block, `Ayaṁ tathāgatassa pacchimā vācā` — "These were the Realized One's last words" (`pi-digha-nikaya.md#^dn16-6-7-4`, `en-digha-nikaya-sujato.md#^dn16-6-7-4`).

- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) *saṅkhārā* has no clean everyday word in any of the six languages. "Conditioned things" / "all that is put together" is plainer than the technical "formations"; Sujato's bare "conditions" is terser but risks reading as "circumstances." Each language should use its standard dharma term (saṃskāra / 有為法·因緣和合 / འདུ་བྱེད) in plain phrasing rather than coining. (b) *sampādetha* is "accomplish / complete / strive on" — an instruction to *finish the work*, slightly stronger than a generic "practise"; reviewers may prefer the completion sense to be explicit. (c) The address frame `handa dāni, bhikkhave, āmantayāmi vo` ("Come now, mendicants, I say to you all," `^dn16-6-7-2`) is **not** part of the shipped quote; the shipped block is the self-contained sentence at `^dn16-6-7-3`. (d) The plural imperative is addressed to monastics but the instruction is universal; render inclusively, without "monks."

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **diligence** (*appamāda*) — distinct from Day 66's `heedfulness` framing (Udānavarga ch.4, which turns on "the deathless" and is flagged for replacement) and from Day 15's `effort` (Dhp 25): this one pairs diligence directly with impermanence and carries no term needing a glossary. Fills the long-owed **Pali prose-Nikāya** slot (log running balance) — the first DN card in the vault. Also closes the ⬜ **Nirvana Day** acquisition gap listed in `occasions.md` ("the Buddha's last words … acquire + rail"), so `occasions: [nirvana-day, vesak, saga-dawa]`. Gate checks: buddhavacana (the Buddha's own final address to the monks, `^dn16-6-7-1`); one self-contained sentence, quoted whole; intelligible with no backstory; sober in subject but explicitly offers the way through (`selection-criteria.md` §2, "offer a way through") — the second clause *is* the remedy; not a proclamation of attainment. Tone handling per `occasions.md`: frame as the call to diligence, not as mourning. Length: en ~66 chars, comfortably inside the budget in all six languages. License: Sujato CC0.
