## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded on the verbatim Chinese (`zh-dharmapada.md#^lgT04p0562a1301`) and its Pali parallel (Dhp 2, Sujato):

1. **中心念善** — "when the heart/mind within holds what is good" (念善 = a wholesome state of mind/intention; the Pali is *manasā pasannena*, a clear/bright mind).
2. **即言即行** — "and one then speaks and acts [from it]."
3. **福樂自追，如影隨形** — "blessing and joy follow of themselves, like a shadow that follows the body."

The teaching: your inner state is not a passive result of what happens to you; the heart you speak and act from is what your experience gives back. Act from a good heart and gladness follows you as surely as your shadow. It speaks *to* the reader about their own day, offers a way through, and holds up no elite.

**Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here):**
- **念善 / a good heart** — ships as "a good heart" (warm, plain), not "wholesome mental states" (clinical) or "pure intention" (Sujato's phrase is fine but "good heart" is warmer for a lay card).
- **福樂 → "joy"** — ordinary gladness; not "merit" and not "blessings" (reads transactional).
- **如影隨形 → "like a shadow"** — keep the shadow image; it is the whole force of the verse.
- The evil-mind twin (`罪苦自追，車轢于轍`, suffering follows like the cart-wheel the ox) is **not** shipped — this is the bright half only. (Day 5 shipped Dhp 1, the dark twin, from the Pali; this bright twin is fresh and complementary.)
