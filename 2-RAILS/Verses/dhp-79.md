## Source Text
![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-dhammapada.md#^79]]

> Dhammapīti sukhaṁ seti, / vippasannena cetasā; / Ariyappavedite dhamme, / sadā ramati paṇḍito.

**Boundary ✓.** One complete Dhp stanza. **Buddhavacana gate ✓** (Dhammapada, corpus-level). No first-person, no "Buddha."

## Authoritative Renderings
### Bhikkhu Sujato — English (CC0)
![[1-SOURCES/Translations/en-dhammapada-sujato.md#^79]]

Sujato: "Through joy in the teaching you sleep at ease, with clear and confident heart. An astute person always delights in the teaching proclaimed by the Noble One."

## Disambiguated Meaning
One who takes joy in the teaching (`dhammapīti`) rests at ease, with a bright and confident heart (`vippasannena cetasā`); the wise always delight in it (1-SOURCES/Translations/en-dhammapada-sujato.md#^79). Gladness, not duty, is the note.

**⚑⚑ Rendering choice flagged — "the teaching" is named, and this brushes the "don't make the card devotional" screen (card-types.md B).** The card's subject *is* joy in the Dhamma, so `dhamma` cannot be removed the way "Buddha" can. It ships as **"the teaching"** (plain, not "the Dhamma"), and the epithet `ariyappavedite` ("proclaimed by the Noble One") is **dropped** to keep the line about the reader's gladness rather than about who proclaimed it. Offered with this flag and chosen by Evan (2026-08-31). A reviewer should confirm it reads as gladness, not devotion.

**⚑ `sukhaṁ seti` = "rests/lives at ease."** Literally "sleeps happily"; rendered "lives at ease" to avoid colliding with the several sleep-at-ease cards (Days 101, 106) and because the point is a settled gladness, not sleep.
