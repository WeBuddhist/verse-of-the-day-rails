## Disambiguated Meaning
Four pādas, grounded in the Tibetan source (`bo-toh313.md#^p326`), with 84000's English consulted as a meaning reference only:

1. **`སེམས་ཅན་ཀུན་དང་སྲོག་ཆགས་ཀུན`** — "all **beings** (`སེམས་ཅན`, those with minds) and all **creatures** (`སྲོག་ཆགས`, those with life)."
2. **`འབྱུང་པོ་ཀུན་དང་ཐམས་ཅད་ཀྱང`** — "and all **living things** (`འབྱུང་པོ`) and absolutely everything." Lines 1–2 are a **fourfold sweep** using three near-synonyms plus a totalising `ཐམས་ཅད` — the genre's way of leaving nothing out. It is emphasis, not taxonomy.
3. **`བདེ་བ་འབའ་ཞིག་གྲུབ་གྱུར་ཏེ`** — "may they attain **happiness alone** (`བདེ་བ་འབའ་ཞིག`) — nothing but happiness."
4. **`ཐམས་ཅད་ཉོན་མོངས་མེད་པར་ཤོག`** — "may all be **without `ཉོན་མོངས`**." See flag (a).

This is an **aspiration**, not an instruction. The reader is not told to cultivate anything; they are handed a wish, already formed, that they can simply make. The `ཤོག` optative is the whole grammatical mood of the card.

- **⚑ Textual-safety note — this is why lines 1–4 and not the whole stanza.** 84000 flags variants at notes **[n13] and [n14]**, and **both fall on pādas 5–6**. Lines 1–4 carry **no variant notes at all**. So the shorter quote is not only the card-length choice, it is the textually safest span in the stanza. Recorded because Toh 313's *other* card (Day 102) sits in the same block and did have an emendation problem — see below.
- **⚑ Same-block precedent, ruled by Evan (2026-08-03).** **Day 102 (`toh313-past-future`) ships a different stanza from this same block `^p326`.** In proposing the two I asserted that only one could run. **Evan overruled it: "totally fine to come from the same block. The quotes are different."** Recorded as precedent — **two cards may share a source block provided the quoted verses are genuinely distinct.** The dedupe rule bites on *verses*, not on blocks. The two are spaced five days apart with different themes and different moods (instruction vs. aspiration). Note also that Day 102's card **did** hit an 84000 emendation (`ཕྲད` → `འབྲང`, its note [n6]) while this one is variant-free — so sharing a block does not mean sharing a textual situation, and each rail must check its own footnotes.
- **⚑ Containment flag.** The same block also holds a stanza on **dying tomorrow** (`སང་ཙམ་ཤི་ཡང་སུ་ཤེས་ཀྱིས`) and Death's great army, immediately **before** this one. That is the sober material the calendar has repeatedly rejected. It is not in this quote and must not be reached for.
- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) **`ཉོན་མོངས` ships as "troubled," not "afflicted" and not "the afflictions."** `ཉོན་མོངས` is the technical term for the mental afflictions (*kleśa*), and rendering it technically would fail the no-glossary screen outright — a casual reader does not know "afflictions." "Troubled" is the ordinary-language cost of keeping the card readable, and it is a real reduction. **This is the card's most significant rendering decision and the first thing a reviewer should test.** (b) The **fourfold sweep of lines 1–2 is compressed to three items** in English ("all beings, all creatures, all that lives") — the three Tibetan near-synonyms have no three distinct English equivalents, and a fourth totalising clause would read as padding. Flagged as a compression of wording, not of content. (c) `བདེ་བ་འབའ་ཞིག` is **"only happiness"** — the exclusive `འབའ་ཞིག` should survive, since "may they be happy" is a weaker wish than "may they find *nothing but* happiness." (d) The optative `ཤོག` must stay an optative in every language — "may…" — never an imperative or a statement.

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **loving-kindness** — recurring, last run **Day 72** (2026-09-03, DĀ 23), thirty-five cards back, with Days 10 and 64 earlier. All three prior cards are *teachings about* loving-kindness; this one is loving-kindness **being performed** — a wish the reader makes rather than a claim about the practice. That is the fresh angle.

**Found by the Kangyur 13–26 kB scan** (2 usable candidates plus this alternate, from 23 files).

Screens: **self-applying, no inference step** ✓ — it hands the reader something to do this second, with nothing to derive. **One idea** ✓ — *may everything alive be happy*. **Warm** ✓ — among the warmest cards in the corpus. **Public-facing** ✓ — a newcomer can make this wish with no background whatsoever. **No image** ✓. Not sober ✓ (the death stanza next door is excluded) · no deity frame ✓ · not a proclamation of attainment ✓ · does not undercut a teacher ✓.

**⚑ Genre note — this is the second card in this batch that is not a teaching.** Day 104 (Toh 292) is a blessing the reader *receives*; this is an aspiration the reader *makes*. Both are new forms for this calendar, and **Evan's decision on Day 104's genre question applies here too.** If blessings and aspirations are ruled out of scope for a daily card, both should be pulled together.
