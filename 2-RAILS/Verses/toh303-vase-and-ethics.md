⚑ **The anchor is folio-granular, not verse-granular.** `^p256` holds the whole
of Degé vol. 72's closing folio — the entire `ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས` verse run plus the
sūtra's colophon. The couplet this rail is built on, verbatim from that block:

> ཇི་ལྟར་བུམ་པ་བཟང་པོ་རིན་ཆེན་སྣོད། །དེ་བཞིན་ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས་ཆོས་རྣམས་སྐྱེད་པའི་གཞི།

**Boundary verified in the Degé, not in the English.** The couplet is a complete
`ཇི་ལྟར` / `དེ་བཞིན` simile pair, closed on both sides by `།`. The pada before it
is the *negative* twin of a different image (`ཇི་ལྟར་རྐང་མེད་ལམ་འཇུག་ག་ལ་ནུས།
།དེ་བཞིན་ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས་མེད་ན་ཐར་མི་འགྱུར།` — no feet, no road); the pada after it is
this image's own negative twin (`ཇི་ལྟར་བུམ་ཆག་རིན་ཆེན་སྣོད་མི་རུང་།` — a broken
vase holds nothing). Cutting at the positive couplet is a real boundary, and it
is the cut that keeps the card affirmative. **Do not extend the quote into the
broken-vase line.**

**Buddhavacana gate ✓ — verified in the source, both ends.** The sūtra opens
`འདི་སྐད་བདག་གིས་ཐོས་པ་དུས་གཅིག་ན། བཅོམ་ལྡན་འདས་མཉན་དུ་ཡོད་པ་ན...` (thus have I
heard; the Blessed One at Śrāvastī, Jeta's Grove) and closes
`བཅོམ་ལྡན་འདས་ཀྱིས་དེ་སྐད་ཅེས་བཀའ་སྩལ་ནས། །དགེ་སློང་དེ་དག་ཡི་རངས་ཏེ།` (the
Blessed One having spoken thus, those monks rejoiced). The verse run is the
Buddha's own teaching to monks throughout. No `ང་` or `བདག་` anywhere in the
couplet — **first-person screen ✓**.

## Disambiguated Meaning

A fine vase is what makes it possible to keep precious things; without it they
have nowhere to sit. In the same way, `ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས` is not itself the good
qualities — it is the **ground that generates and holds them**
(`སྐྱེད་པའི་གཞི`, literally "the basis of producing")
(1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh303.md#^p256). The verse makes ethical conduct
*enabling* rather than restrictive: the container, not the constraint.

**⚑ Rendering choice flagged, not fixed — `ཆོས་རྣམས` here means "qualities,"
not "the Dharma."** This is the one place this card can go badly wrong. `ཆོས`
carries both senses, and a modern reader — especially on the `bo` line — could
hear "moral discipline is the ground that produces *the Dharma*," which is a
different and much bigger claim. 84000 reads it as "good qualities"
(`en-toh303-84000.md`, line 49), and the surrounding run supports that: the
neighbouring padas pair `ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས` with reputation, radiance, being praised,
and a good rebirth — personal qualities, not the teaching itself. **Every
rendering must disambiguate toward "qualities."** Recorded here so a reviewer
audits the choice rather than re-deriving it.

**⚑ Second rendering choice — `ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས` ships at the termbase's warmer end.**
`termbase.md` line 90 gives "ethics, moral conduct." The card ships **"ethical
conduct"** and avoids "moral discipline" (84000's term), which reads punitive
and monastic to a lay reader — the same objection the `saṃvara` row records for
"restraint" (termbase line 124). Keep `ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས` itself in the `bo` line: it is
the source's own word, the termbase term, and current in modern Tibetan.

**⚑ `རིན་ཆེན་སྣོད` is a compressed noun-compound**, literally "precious-thing
vessel." It is not a verb phrase; the couplet has no finite verb at all in
either half. Every rendering therefore has to supply a copula, and the choice of
verb ("holds" / "is a vessel for" / "keeps") is the translator's, not the
source's. Flagged so no reviewer reads "holds" as a claim about the Tibetan.

## Theme & Selection Notes

**Theme:** `ethics-as-ground`. **Card type:** simile — and it is the safe kind,
because the quote **names its own referent** in its second half rather than
leaving the reader to work out what the vase stands for. That is the screen the
vault's simile guidance flags as highest-risk, and this couplet clears it inside
its own four padas.

**Self-contained ✓, intelligible out of context ✓.** Both halves are present;
nothing depends on the surrounding sūtra or on knowing who is being addressed.

**Classical → modern substitutions made in the shipped `bo`** (per the
source-language-ships-modern rule; audit each one rather than re-deriving):
1. Verse metrical breaks `། །` flattened to running prose.
2. `ཇི་ལྟར ... དེ་བཞིན` (classical correlative pair) → `... ནང་བཞིན`, the
   ordinary modern comparative.
3. `རིན་ཆེན་སྣོད` (compressed compound) → `རིན་པོ་ཆེ་འཇོག་ས་བྱེད་པ`, unpacked
   into a plain modern phrase.
4. `ཆོས་རྣམས` → `ཡོན་ཏན་ཡག་པོ་ཚང་མ`, **deliberately disambiguated** to "good
   qualities" so the line cannot be heard as "the Dharma" (see above).
5. `སྐྱེད་པའི་གཞི` → `སྐྱེ་སའི་གཞི་རྟེན་རེད`, with the modern copula `རེད`.

**⚑ The borderline flag this entry carried, and how it was resolved.**
`candidate-pool/tibetan.md` held this entry back unoffered on warmth: it is the
single salvageable unit in a text that is otherwise a wall of "discipline is the
best ornament / the best fragrance / the source of all joy" — the
compressed-parallel-praise shape the vault has rejected before — and the
surrounding register is austere enough that the couplet might read as moralising
by association. **It was offered to Evan with that flag stated and he selected
it (2026-08-31)**, following the Day 122 precedent that a flagged pool entry
belongs in the proposal rather than the bin. The flag is recorded, not erased:
if the card reads as moralising in review, this is the reason why.

**⚑ Adjacency to ship with care — see the day card and `log.md`.** Day 130
(Iti 59) also has ethics in it and also closes on a simile. Evan selected both
in one turn with the adjacency stated. The recommended fix, if the pair reads
repetitive, is to put a non-ethics card between them rather than to drop either.
