## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Chinese source (`zh-digha-agama.md#^pT01p0026a2513`) and the Pali parallel (`pi-digha-nikaya.md#^dn16-6-1-5`; Sujato at the same anchor):

1. **`我成佛來所說經戒`** — "**the teaching and the training I have spoken since I awoke**." `經` = the discourses, `戒` = the training/precepts — exactly the Pali parallel's pair `dhammo ca vinayo ca`. `成佛來` is "since attaining awakening," a time marker on the teaching, not a claim about the speaker's status.
2. **`即是汝護`** — "**that is your 護**." `護` is protection, guarding, shelter — what covers and keeps you. The preceding question uses the fuller `覆護` ("cover and protect"), which fixes the sense: shelter, not defence.
3. **`是汝所持`** — "**that is what you hold / uphold**." `持` is both to hold onto and to keep up (as one keeps precepts), so the phrase is deliberately two-directional: it holds you, you keep it.

The scene is Ānanda's fear of being left with nobody to rely on. The answer relocates the shelter from a person to the teaching, which is already in the listener's hands. **On the card the scene is absent and the sentence still works**, because it names both what the shelter is and whose it is.

- **⚑ THE CHINESE AND THE PALI DIVERGE ON THE KEY WORD, and the card ships the Chinese. Do not flatten this.** The Pali says the teaching will be your **teacher** (`satthā`). The Chinese says it is your **護** (protection/shelter) and `所持` (what you hold). These are different claims: one is about authority, the other about safety. **The Chinese is the source here**, so the card ships shelter-and-support; the Pali is cited to establish the scene, the pairing of discourses-and-training, and that the passage is authentic across recensions. A reviewer must not "correct" the card to "your teacher" on the strength of DN 16.
- **⚑ The sentence boundary is real, and the Pali proves it.** CBETA punctuates `勿造斯觀，我成佛來所說經戒…` with a comma, which could suggest one long sentence and make the quote look like it starts mid-sentence. The Pali parallel punctuates the two as **separate sentences** — `^dn16-6-1-4` "But you should not see it like this." and `^dn16-6-1-5` "The teaching and training…" — so the boundary the quote uses is the recension's own, not an invented cut. `勿造斯觀` ("do not form that view") is excluded because it is a correction of Ānanda's specific fear and, without the fear stated, reads as a rebuke to the reader.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `成佛來` must not put the word "Buddha" on the card.** Literally "since I became a Buddha." The card ships "**since my awakening**", and the modern `zh` ships **`成道以來`** rather than `成佛以來`. This is required by the screen Evan set on 2026-08-03 (Toh 312 was rejected for naming the Buddha in the card text) and it is a real substitution, not a translation nicety — flagged here so it is visible rather than silently smoothed. ⚑ **The residual risk a reviewer should judge:** the card is still in the Buddha's first person, and "since my awakening" keeps him as the source of the shelter. Evan's screen names *the word*; whether it reaches first-person self-reference is the open question recorded in `card-types.md`, and this card sits on that line.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `經戒` ships as "all I have taught," collapsing the pair.** "The discourses and the precepts" or "the teaching and the training" is more faithful to `經` + `戒` and to the Pali `dhammo ca vinayo ca`, but on a card it reads institutional and doubles the length. "All I have taught" holds both. Flagged as a real reduction; the fuller pair survives in the `zh` line (`教法和戒律`), where it costs nothing.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `護` ships as "shelter," `所持` as "support."** "Protection" is more literal for `護` but collocates with security services; "shelter" carries the covering sense of `覆護`. "Support" for `所持` keeps the two-directional hold. The pairing "shelter and support" is deliberately warm and slightly redundant, matching the source's own doubling.
- **Do not import the rest of the block.** It continues into monastic protocol (`聽諸比丘捨小小戒，上下相呼…`) which is out of register and irrelevant to a lay reader. Excluded.

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **the-teaching-as-shelter.** Adjacent cards: `refuge` (Day 60, DĀ 23) and `practice-as-true-offering` (Day 62, also DĀ 2, a different quote). Both of those name the Three Jewels or an offering; neither says *what you already have is enough shelter*, which is this card's content. ⚑ Note Day 60's card contains "I go for refuge to the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Sangha" and is on `card-types.md`'s list of nine cards flagged under the no-naming screen — this card is the same theme family handled the new way, and the contrast is worth a reviewer's eye.

**Gate checks.** *Buddhavacana* — the block opens `佛言` ("the Buddha said") and the sentence is his first-person speech to Ānanda. Not praise *of* him, not a disciple's words. *In scope* — Dīgha Āgama. *Traceable* — `^pT01p0026a2513`. *License* — CBETA **CC BY-NC-SA** (source); Sujato **CC0** (Pali parallel). *Real quote, kept whole* — one complete sentence; the boundary is grounded on the Pali's punctuation, see the flag. *Length* — en 70 (the shortest card in this batch), zh 31. *Fresh* — DĀ 2 supplied Day 62 from the same sūtra but a different anchor and a different teaching, which is permitted precedent (Evan, 2026-08-03).

**Screens.** *Arrives already applied* ✓✓ — "your" twice, in the source itself; this is the strongest card in the batch on this screen. *Lands on one read* ✓ — the deathbed scene is absent and nothing depends on it; the sentence names its own subject and its own beneficiary. *One idea in five words* ✓ — "the teaching is your shelter." *Warm* ✓ — pure consolation, no instruction, no correction. *Dharma, not general life advice* ✓ — its subject is the teaching itself. *No "Buddha" in the card* ✓ **by substitution** — see the flag, and the residual first-person question. *Not sober* ⚑ — the host scene is the Buddha's approaching death, which is why `勿造斯觀` and the surrounding material are excluded; the shipped sentence contains no mortality content and reads as reassurance, but a reviewer who knows the passage will hear the farewell in it. Judged acceptable because the card ships alone; recorded because it is the one place a sober note could enter this batch.

**Batch spacing.** Distinct from contentment (112), the ground of diligence (113), the unshaken mind (114) and steady effort (116). No overlap.

**Shipped-zh policy — modern, not the classical source.** The verbatim CBETA sentence is **the source**; the shipped `zh` is **modern Traditional Chinese**.
