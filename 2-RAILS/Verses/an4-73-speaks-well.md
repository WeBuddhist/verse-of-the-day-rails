## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Pali (`^an4-73-6-1`) and Sujato at the same anchor:

1. **`sappuriso`** — a **true / good person** (`sat` + `purisa`), a standing Pali category for someone of developed character. Not merely "a nice person."
2. **`parassa vaṇṇo`** — **another's good qualities**, their praise or good report.
3. **`taṁ apuṭṭhopi pātu karoti`** — "**makes it plain even when not asked** (`apuṭṭho`)." The unprompted part is the whole content: the behaviour is only diagnostic *because* nobody solicited it.

The teaching identifies a small, observable act as a mark of character. It does not ask the reader to feel differently about anyone; it names a thing to do, and the doing is available immediately.

- **⚑ The *a fortiori* tail is dropped.** `ko pana vādo puṭṭhassa` ("let alone when asked") completes the sentence but adds no content beyond emphasis, and including it makes the card top-heavy. Stopping at the clause boundary is permitted (`selection-criteria.md` §2). Note the contrast with **Day 93**, where a structurally identical `何況` tail was *kept* because there the small/large comparison carried the teaching; here the unprompted case *is* the teaching and the tail is decoration.
- **⚑ The positive limb only.** AN 4.73 lists four marks of a `sappurisa` and their negative mirrors. `^an4-73-5-3` is this one's mirror ("doesn't speak ill of another") and reads as admonishment; `^an4-73-7-1`/`8-1` concern self-deprecation and would fail the warmth screen. **Take `6-1` alone.** Shipping the full four would be the parallel-claims shape that got Day 87 rejected.
- **⚑ This is the batch's closest call on the dharma screen, and it should be watched.** In the same proposal Evan rejected AN 2.33 (*"Parents give their children so much…"*) with *"it's just about parenting. Not so much about dharma."* A card about speaking well of people is adjacent to that risk — it could read as etiquette. **What keeps it on the dharma side:** `sappurisa` is a doctrinal category, not a compliment, and the sutta's frame is explicitly *what marks a true person* — a question about character formation, which is practice. Recorded so a reviewer can disagree knowingly rather than discover the tension later.
- **Rendering choices flagged.** (a) `sappurisa` ships as **"a good person"** — plain, and matching the register already accepted on Day 41 (AN 2.32, `sappuriso`) and Day 99. "A true person" is Sujato's and more literal but reads slightly odd cold; "a person of integrity" over-translates. (b) `apuṭṭhopi` ships as **"even when nobody asks"** rather than "unasked" (terse) or "without being asked" (passive and flatter) — the point is the absence of any prompt. (c) `parassa vaṇṇo` ships as **"speaks well of others"**, plural, generalising the source's singular `parassa` so the card is not about one particular person. Flagged as a small widening. (d) `Puna caparaṁ` ("furthermore") is a list connective and is dropped.
