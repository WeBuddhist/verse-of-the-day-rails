The four padas, verbatim, for reference while reading this rail:

> Sīlaṁ samādhi paññā ca, / yassa ete subhāvitā; / Atikkamma māradheyyaṁ, / ādiccova virocatī"ti.

**Boundary ✓.** The quatrain is the whole of Iti 59's verse portion: it opens at
`^iti59-3-1` immediately after the formula `Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati:`
(`^iti59-2-6`) and closes at `^iti59-3-4` with the quotation-closing `"ti`.
Nothing precedes or follows it inside the verse.

**Buddhavacana gate ✓.** `Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca` — "this is what the Blessed
One said" — stands at `^iti59-2-5`, directly before the verse
(1-SOURCES/Text/pi-itivuttaka.md#^iti59-2-5). Itivuttaka is buddhavacana at
corpus level besides.

**First-person screen ✓.** The grammatical subject is `yassa` — "whoever," an
indefinite relative. The Buddha does not appear in the verse in any person, and
the word "Buddha" does not occur.

## Disambiguated Meaning

The three trainings are named as a set and qualified by one word, `subhāvitā`
("well developed") — the claim is not that one has ethics, stillness and wisdom,
but that one has developed them **well, and together**
(1-SOURCES/Translations/en-itivuttaka-sujato.md#^iti59-3-2). The result is
stated twice: such a person has gone past `māradheyya`, Māra's range or
territory, and shines like the sun
(1-SOURCES/Translations/en-itivuttaka-sujato.md#^iti59-3-3).

**The prose that precedes the verse generalises in the verse's favour, not
against it.** At `^iti59-2-3` the same three are described as the *adept's*
(`asekha`) aggregates and are predicated of a `bhikkhu`; the verse drops both
the monastic subject and the adept qualifier and replaces them with the open
`yassa` (1-SOURCES/Text/pi-itivuttaka.md#^iti59-2-3). **The card therefore ships
the verse, not the prose** — the verse is the form in which the teaching is
addressed to anyone.

**⚑ Rendering choice flagged — `samādhi`.** `termbase.md` line 91 gives
"concentration, meditative stillness"; Sujato uses "immersion." The card ships
**"stillness of mind."** Reasoning: "concentration" reads as effortful mental
strain to a lay reader, which is close to the opposite of what is meant, and
"immersion" is Sujato's idiolect and not current outside his translations. This
takes the termbase's **second** listed option, so it is inside the row rather
than a departure from it. ⚑ Note the `santi` row's caution (termbase line 105)
runs the other way and must be respected: *do not* let "stillness" leak back
into peace contexts as a rendering of `santi`. The two rows stay distinct.

**⚑⚑ Rendering choice flagged for a curator, not settled here — `māradheyya`.**
The compound is `māra` + `dheyya` (domain, range, what is subject to someone).
The card ships **"Māra's reach."** Three things a reviewer should weigh:
1. **Māra is not a Brahmā-class problem.** The screen that killed Toh 315 is
   about *culture-specific deity or Vedic frames*. Māra is specifically
   Buddhist and is present under his own name in all three of this vault's
   canons — `བདུད` in Tibetan, 魔 in Chinese — so he is recognisable to the
   whole audience rather than to one part of it.
2. **But he is still a named mythological figure**, and the standing screen is
   that a card must land in one second. A reader who does not know the name
   gets an image of something being escaped, which is most of the meaning, but
   not all of it.
3. **The alternative was considered and not taken:** rendering the sense
   ("beyond temptation's reach") drops a real term the tradition names, which
   is the paraphrase the ecumenical-wording rule exists to prevent.
**No termbase row for `māra` existed**; one has been added rather than deciding
this per-verse, per `verse-rail` rule 3.

**⚑ `ādiccova virocati` is a simile, and it names its own referent** — the
person who has developed the three is the one who shines. It does not need the
reader to supply the comparison. This keeps the card inside the safe-simile
screen even though the card type is `teaching`.

## Theme & Selection Notes

**Theme:** `threefold-training`. Fresh instance — the vault's existing
threefold-training card is Day 17 (Toh 282), 113 days back, from a different
canon and without the sun image.

**Card type: `teaching`, deliberately, though it closes on a simile.** The
sentence's work is the instruction (develop these three well); the sun is its
final clause, not its structure. This matters for sequencing: Day 129 is a
`simile` card, and labelling this one `simile` too would put two of the same
type back to back, which `card-types.md` rule 2 forbids. **The label is honest
rather than convenient — but a reviewer who disagrees should reorder rather
than relabel.**

**⚑ Adjacency flagged, and it is a real one.** Day 129 (Toh 303) is
`ethics-as-ground`; this card's first word is ethics and it also ends in an
image. Evan selected both in one turn on 2026-08-31 with the adjacency stated
in the proposal, and separately said in the same turn that the calendar has
**too many ethics topics** — so this pair should be read as approved material,
not as an approved *sequence*. **Recommended fix: put a non-ethics card between
them** once the next batch produces one, moving this card to Day 131. Recorded
rather than acted on, per the vault's practice of not renumbering unasked.

**Why it is not a proclamation of attainment.** The verse describes what
develops and what follows from it in the third person and in the open (`yassa`).
It is not a first-person announcement of a finished state, which is the form
`selection-criteria.md` §2 rules out.
