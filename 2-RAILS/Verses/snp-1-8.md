> Mātā yathā niyaṁ puttam / Āyusā ekaputtamanurakkhe; / Evampi sabbabhūtesu, / mānasaṁ bhāvaye aparimāṇaṁ.

**Boundary ✓.** One complete verse (Snp 1.8, verse 7), a single `yathā … evampi` simile sentence. The next verse (8-1…8-4, "with love for the whole world…") is a separate stanza and is not shipped — verse 7 is whole on its own.

**Buddhavacana gate ✓.** The Karaṇīyamettasutta is the Buddha's own teaching, buddhavacana at corpus level (the sutta is the instruction beginning `Karaṇīyamatthakusalena`, `^snp1-8-1-1`). No first-person, no "Buddha" in the verse.

## Disambiguated Meaning
The mother-and-only-child image sets the *degree* of care, not a restriction of its object: as a mother would guard her one child with her life, so one should develop (`bhāvaye`) a heart that is boundless (`aparimāṇa`) toward all beings (`sabbabhūtesu`) (1-SOURCES/Translations/en-sutta-nipata-sujato.md#^snp1-8-7-4). The point is the *extension* — the same fierce protectiveness, widened without limit.

**⚑ mettā ships per termbase** (loving-kindness / 慈 / བྱམས་པ / मैत्री), never "love" — the standard-term rule. The verse's own word here is `mānasaṁ … aparimāṇaṁ` (a boundless heart/mind); `mettā` is the sutta's title-theme and the termbase term the renderings carry.
