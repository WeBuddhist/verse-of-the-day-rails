## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Pali (`^sn10-2-4-1` … `-4-4`) and Sujato at the same anchors:

1. **`manasā ce pasannena`** — "**if with a mind that is `pasanna`**." `pasanna` is clear, settled, bright, confident — the word used of water that has become clear, and of a mind that has settled into trust. Not "pure" and not "pleased."
2. **`yadaññamanusāsati`** — "**when one instructs / advises another.**" `anusāsati` covers instructing, advising, guiding. On a card it must not read as lecturing.
3. **`na tena hoti saṁyutto`** — "**one is not bound by that.**" `saṁyutta` is tied, yoked, fettered — the standard word for the bond that attachment creates.
4. **`yānukampā anuddayā`** — "**namely, sympathy and tenderness.**" `anukampā` is fellow-feeling, being moved by another's condition; `anuddayā` is tenderness, care, solicitude. The two are the subject of "not bound": *it is these that do not bind.*

The reframe answers a question the reader is actually asking: **does caring about people entangle me?** The answer is no, provided the heart it comes from is clear. It does not tell the reader to care more, and it does not warn them about attachment; it removes a fear that was making them pull back.

- **⚑ THE PALI IS IMPERSONAL AND THE SECOND PERSON IS SUJATO'S. Disclose on any reuse.** The stanza has a conditional `ce` with a third-person singular verb (`anusāsati`), and `na tena hoti saṁyutto` has no expressed subject; `yānukampā anuddayā` is a bare nominal phrase. **There is no pronoun of any person in the Pali.** Sujato supplies "you" and "your." This is the standing Nikāya finding — Day 103, AN 5.34, MN 96, and now Day 117 (SN 47.19) and this card. It is the *weaker* form of the disclosure, since we are following the authoritative translation rather than going beyond it, as Day 114 did.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `anusāsati` ships as "guide," not "instruct" or "advise."** Sujato has "instruct," which is accurate and reads schoolmasterly in modern English; "advise" is transactional. "Guide someone" is what a person would say about helping another, and it keeps the card out of a teacher-pupil frame the reader may not be in.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `pasanna` ships as "a clear and confident heart."** Sujato has "a mind clear and confident," which is right; "heart" is used because in every other language on this card the word will be the heart-mind term, and because "a clear mind" in English suggests concentration rather than settledness. Flagged as a small register move.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `anukampā anuddayā` ships as "your kindness and care," not "compassion and empathy."** ⚑ **Deliberately NOT "compassion":** the termbase reserves that for *karuṇā*, and `anukampā` is a different word; using "compassion" here would silently claim a termbase row this verse does not use. "Empathy" (Sujato) is a modern psychological term that reads clinically on a card. "Kindness and care" is plain and keeps the pair. **A termbase row for `anukampā` should be added if this verse's family recurs** — noted rather than coined, per `verse-rail` rule 3.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `na tena hoti saṁyutto` ships as "will not tie you down."** "Does not create attachments" (Sujato) is accurate but abstract; the point is felt, not doctrinal, and `saṁyutta` is a rope word. "Tie you down" is the collocation an English speaker would use about caring costing them their freedom.
- **The spirit and the challenge are excluded, and the card does not need them.** A yakkha named Sakka-in-name-only tells the Buddha that since he is fully freed it is "not a good idea" for him to be instructing others; stanzas 3 and 4 are the Buddha's reply. Stanza 3 carries the vocative and the rebuke; **stanza 4 alone is the answer and it stands complete.**

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **care-does-not-bind**, new tag.

**Why this was missed by two previous scans.** Not a pronoun problem — a **speaker-attribution** problem. SN 10 is the yakkha saṁyutta, which the vault's own notes flag as almost entirely non-buddhavacana, so the whole block gets discarded wholesale by a speaker screen. **But the Buddha's replies inside it are his own words.** Worth recording as a method note: the *saṁyutta*-level judgement "this collection is mostly not the Buddha" throws away his replies along with the challenges. The same is true of SN 1 (deities) and SN 11 (Sakka).

**Dedupe — checked against the English line of all 114 shipped cards.** Nearest is **Day 45** (Diamond Sūtra, *"Give freely, without dwelling on anything at all"*) — an instruction on the *manner* of a material gift, telling the reader not to fixate while giving. This card asks and answers a different question, in the reader's favour: *does caring about people entangle me?* No. Also checked **Day 39** (Toh 122, cultivate great compassion) and **Day 70** (EĀ, tend the sick) — both commend compassion without saying anything about its cost; **Day 100** (giving without expecting return); and **Day 117** (SN 47.19), which is the *reciprocity* of care, not its freedom. ⚑ Day 117 is the closest in feel and is six days earlier — see batch spacing.

**Gate checks.** *Buddhavacana* — verified in the file: the spirit addresses the Buddha in verse at `^sn10-2-1-2`/`2-1`…`2-4`, and stanzas 3 and 4 are the Buddha's reply; this is stanza 4. Not praise, not about him, no disciple involved. *In scope* — Saṁyutta Nikāya. *Traceable* — `^sn10-2-4-1` … `-4-4`. *License* — SuttaCentral/Sujato **CC0**. *Real quote, kept whole* — one complete stanza. *Length* — en 100. *Fresh* — SN 10.2 has never been used, and the yakkha saṁyutta has never supplied a card.

**Screens.** *Arrives already applied* ✓ (via Sujato's second person — see the flag). *Lands on one read* ✓ — no yakkha, no Sakka, no doctrinal term; the condition and the reassurance are both inside the stanza. *One idea in five words* ✓ — "caring does not bind you." *Warm* ✓ — it lifts a burden rather than adding one. *Dharma, not general life advice* ✓ — the relation between compassion and non-attachment is core doctrine, and `saṁyutta` is the technical bond term; the card is not a self-care tip. *No "Buddha" in the card* ✓. *Not sober* ✓. *No self-reliance framed as excluding others* ✓ — it is the opposite. *Not a proclamation of attainment* ✓ — note the *spirit* makes an attainment claim about the Buddha in stanza 2, and that stanza is excluded.

**Batch spacing.** ⚑ **Day 117 (SN 47.19) is the nearest neighbour in feel** — both concern caring for others and what it costs you — and it is three days before this card. They make different claims (117: self-care and other-care produce each other; this: care from a clear heart creates no bond) and use different vocabulary, but a reviewer should see them together and may want this one held. The rest of the batch this card was proposed in was declined by Evan, so the only other card built alongside it is **Day 121 (Toh 176, discouragement)**, which is a different ground.
