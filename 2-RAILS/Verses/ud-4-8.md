## Disambiguated Meaning
On hearing a harsh word spoken (`sutvāna vākyaṁ pharusaṁ udīritaṁ`), one should endure it (`adhivāsaye`) with a heart that holds no ill will (`aduṭṭhacitto`) (1-SOURCES/Translations/en-udana-sujato.md#^ud4-8-19-4). Not "don't feel it" — receive it without the heart turning to anger.

**⚑ `adhivāsana` renders as "endure / bear," not "restraint"** (the `saṃvara` termbase caution: "restraint" reads as self-denial). "Meet it / bear it with a heart free of anger" keeps it active and warm.

## Theme & Selection Notes
**Theme:** `patience-with-harsh-words`. Distinct from the anger cards (Days 3, 7, 65, 106): those are about *your* anger arising; this is specifically about *receiving someone else's* harsh speech. Card type: **teaching**. Casual-audience fit ✓: everyone gets spoken to sharply; very relatable.

**⚑ Pairs with Day 136 (AN 5.198, kind speech) — deliberately spaced.** 136 is how you speak; 138 is how you receive harsh speech. One day between (Day 137) keeps them from reading as a speech cluster. Flagged for the curator.
