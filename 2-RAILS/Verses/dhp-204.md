## Disambiguated Meaning

Four things, each named as the highest of its kind: health is the highest gain
(`ārogyaparamā lābhā`); contentment is the highest wealth (`santuṭṭhiparamaṁ
dhanaṁ`); a trusted friend is the highest kin (`vissāsaparamā ñāti`); and
`nibbāna` is the highest happiness (`nibbānaṁ paramaṁ sukhaṁ`)
(1-SOURCES/Translations/en-dhammapada-sujato.md#^204). The verse reframes
"wealth" and "gain" away from possessions toward states one can actually have:
being well, being content, being trusted.

**⚑⚑ The load-bearing rendering decision — `nibbāna` ships as "peace," not named.**
`card-types.md` screen B keeps the shipped card text clear of "Buddha," and by
the same reasoning the vault avoids making `nibbāna` the card's named subject
(the card is for the reader's own life, not a doctrinal term to decode). But
the fourth pada **cannot be dropped** — "kept whole" forbids shipping three of
four padas. So `nibbāna` is **rendered**, plainly, as **"peace"** — a defensible
modern gloss (nibbāna as the peace of release / cooling), and the same move made
for `nibbuta` elsewhere. ⚑ This is a real interpretive choice and a reviewer may
object on two grounds: (a) "peace" under-translates `nibbāna`, flattening the
final and highest term to something ordinary; (b) it may be *better* to under-
translate here precisely so the card stays plain. **Flagged, not settled** —
this is the single decision the reviewer must rule on for this card. Do not
silently restore "Nibbāna" or "extinguishment."

**⚑ `vissāsa` = "trust / the trusted one," rendered "a trusted friend."** Sujato
has "trust ... the ultimate family." `vissāsa` is confidence/trust; `ñāti` is
kin/relative. "A trusted friend is the closest kin" keeps both nouns and reads
warmly; flagged so a reviewer sees "friend" is supplied to carry `vissāsa` in a
person, not invented.

## Theme & Selection Notes

**Theme:** `contentment`. Third contentment card (Day 20 Dhp 200, Day 112 T389),
but the freshest angle: 200 is "we who have nothing live happily"; 112 is
"content though poor is rich"; this one **ranks contentment against health,
friendship and peace** rather than against poverty. Distinct enough to run.

**⚑⚑ A LIST IS FINE — this card corrects an over-broad rejection (Evan, 2026-08-31).** Dhp 204 was
listed in `candidate-pool/rejected.md` and `log.md` as **"presumed dead"** under the **"one idea, one
image"** screen (Evan's Day 87 ruling), alongside Snp 3.3, Iti 93 and others. **Evan has now drawn the
distinction that entry missed:** *"There's nothing wrong with a list. That's different than a bunch of
mixed ideas in one quote."*

- **Dhp 204 is a list, not mixed ideas.** Its four padas are one frame — `parama` ("the highest X") —
  repeated four times, all answering a single question: *what is the best of each kind of good?* That is
  a coherent parallel list under one idea (the real riches are states, not things), which is exactly what
  the Day 87 verse (Snp 1.10: faith / practice / truth / wisdom — four different subjects, no frame) was
  **not**. The one-idea rule targets *unrelated claims stacked to fill the budget*, not *a unified list*.
- **What the rule actually says, corrected:** the test is not "does the card contain more than one item"
  but "do the items cohere under one idea or image." A parallel list with a shared frame passes; a stack
  of mixed claims fails. `card-types.md`'s "can you name the single image in five words?" test should be
  read as "can you name the single *idea*" — for Dhp 204 the answer is yes: *the highest kind of each
  good.*
- **It has never shipped** (no prior card or rail), so this revives it cleanly rather than duplicating.
- **⚑ For the curation docs (a human owns these):** `candidate-pool/rejected.md` line ~322 and the
  `log.md` running-balance note that both name Dhp 204 (and by the same logic Snp 3.3 / Udānavarga ch.8,
  the fourfold speech formulas, and Iti 93, the poison/antidote pairs) as dead under the one-idea rule
  should be **re-scoped** — those may be legitimate unified lists too, and are worth re-examining rather
  than left "presumed dead." Flagging for Evan, not editing `4-SYSTEM/` or auto-reviving them.

**The rendering opportunity that follows.** Because the four cohere under "the real riches are states,
not things," a rendering that lets that frame carry them will read as one idea rather than four bests
recited flatly. The renderings below aim for that.

**One-second test.** As a list it reads fast because the frame repeats — "the
highest X, the highest Y" — so the reader is not holding four unrelated things
but one pattern four times. The only lift is the fourth pada ("peace the highest
joy"), a step up in abstraction from the concrete first three; the rendering
keeps it plain ("peace") to hold the card at one register.
