## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded on the verbatim Tibetan (`bo-udanavarga.md#^p491`):

1. **མཛའ་བཤེས་ཀུན་དང་གྲོགས་ཀུན་དང་། །འབྱུང་པོ་ཀུན་ལ་བརྩེ་བ་དང་།** — "cherishing all friends and companions, and [holding] tenderness (བརྩེ་བ) toward all beings (འབྱུང་པོ, *bhūta*)."
2. **བྱམས་པའི་སེམས་ནི་བསྒོམས་བྱས་ན།** — "if one cultivates (བསྒོམས) a mind of loving-kindness (བྱམས་པ, *mettā*)."
3. **བདེ་བ་ཤིན་ཏུ་འཕེལ་བར་འགྱུར།** — "one's wellbeing/happiness (བདེ་བ) grows exceedingly."

The teaching, addressed to the reader: opening the heart is not only for others' sake; as you cultivate loving-kindness toward everyone around you and every living being, your own wellbeing grows with it. It offers a way in for someone who feels hardened or guarded, and holds up no distant elite.

**⚑ Term note (Evan, 2026-11-19): this is loving-kindness (`བྱམས་པ` / mettā), not compassion (`སྙིང་རྗེ` / karuṇā).** Evan asked for a compassion verse and chose this one knowingly; the theme is tagged **loving-kindness** honestly, not `compassion`. The verse does also carry `བརྩེ་བ` (tenderness/cherishing) toward beings, which leans compassion-ward, but its core cultivated quality is `བྱམས་པ`.

**Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here):**
- **བྱམས་པ → "loving-kindness"** per `termbase.md` (mettā = loving-kindness / 慈愛 / མैत्री), never "love".
- **བརྩེ་བ → "cherish / tenderness"** — the warm, everyday cherishing; do not collapse it into "compassion" (that is `སྙིང་རྗེ`, a different term) or "affection" (risks the Dhp-212 withdraw-affection misreading; here the move is toward others, not away, so it is safe, but keep the wording as active cherishing).
- **བདེ་བ → "your own happiness / wellbeing"** — ordinary wellbeing; the reader-facing payoff, kept explicit ("your own").
