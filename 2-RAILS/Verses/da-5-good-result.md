## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Chinese source (`zh-digha-agama.md#^pT01p0037a1704`):

1. **`夫行善法必有善報`** — "one who practises **wholesome things** (`善法`) **certainly** (`必`) has a **good result** (`善報`)." `必` is the load-bearing word: this is a statement of reliability, not of likelihood.
2. **`行清白行必有白報`** — "one who lives a **clean and blameless life** (`清白行`) certainly has a **clean result** (`白報`)." The Chinese repeats the structure and plays on `清白` / `白` — the conduct and its result share the same word, so the result is not a reward *for* the conduct but is *of the same kind as* it.

The teaching's move is to answer a doubt rather than to issue an instruction. Its subject is the reliability of the connection: what you do produces its own kind of outcome, dependably, and the parallel wording makes the point that the outcome resembles the act.

- **⚑ The caste argument is deliberately outside the quote.** In context, the Buddha is dismantling the brahmin claim that birth determines destiny — the sentence continues "if this result belonged to brahmins alone…" and goes on to apply the same law across all four castes. That argument is the sutta's purpose but it is **not** in the quote, and no rendering should import it. Without it the sentence stands as a plain statement about action and result, which is what makes it a card.
- **⚑ This replaces the pool's existing entry, which had the negative half.** `candidate-pool/chinese.md`'s `da-5-b` cites `^pT01p0037a0105` — correct, but that anchor holds the **negative** twin (`夫不善行有不善報…`, "unwholesome conduct has an unwholesome result"). The positive form twelve lines later is strictly better on the warmth screen. Same move as Day 93's negative-mirror drop. Pool corrected.
- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) **`必` must survive** as "is sure to" or "certainly" — dropping it turns a statement of reliability into a vague encouragement, and the reliability *is* the teaching. (b) `善報` is a **result**, not a "reward" — "reward" implies someone granting it, which imports an agent the text does not have. (c) The **`清白`/`白` echo should be preserved** where the target language allows ("lives cleanly… a clean result"), since the resemblance between act and outcome is the second clause's whole point; where it cannot be preserved, say so in QA rather than silently flattening it. (d) `清白行` is **blameless, upright living** — not ritual or bodily purity, and not "innocence."
