> 佛告須深：「受汝悔過，汝當具說：『我昔愚癡、不善、無智，於正法、律盜密出家，今日悔過，自見罪、自知罪，於當來世律儀成就，功德增長，終不退減。』所以者何？凡人有罪，自見、自知而悔過者，於當來世律儀成就，功德增長，終不退減。」

**The shipped sentence, verbatim:**
> 凡人有罪，自見、自知而悔過者，於當來世律儀成就，功德增長，終不退減。
> *(verbatim substring of `zh-samyukta-agama.md#^pT02p0097c1714`, quoted whole. ## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Chinese source (`zh-samyukta-agama.md#^pT02p0097c1714`) and the Pali parallel (`^sn12-70-29-4`; Sujato at the same anchor):

1. **`凡人有罪`** — "**when anyone has done wrong**." `凡` marks it as fully general, which is what lifts the sentence out of Susīma's particular case.
2. **`自見、自知而悔過者`** — "**one who sees it for themselves, knows it for themselves, and owns it**." Three verbs, and the doubled `自` is load-bearing: the recognition is the person's own, not something extracted from them. `悔過` is to acknowledge and turn from a fault, not merely to feel bad about it.
3. **`於當來世律儀成就`** — "**in time to come their conduct becomes established**." `律儀` is *saṃvara*, restraint or settled conduct (termbase row added with this card).
4. **`功德增長，終不退減`** — "**goodness grows, and in the end does not fall away or diminish**." `終不退減` is emphatic and final.

So: **the single act of seeing your own fault honestly is not the end of something, it is the start of a growth that does not reverse.** The card addresses the person who *has* the fault, and offers no method and no penance — only what that one honest look sets in motion.

- **⚑ The Pali parallel supplies a fourth element the Chinese does not, and the card follows the Chinese.** SN 12.70 has three moves — *recognize a mistake for what it is, deal with it properly, and commit to restraint in the future* — where the Chinese has recognition (`自見、自知`) plus `悔過`, and then treats settled conduct (`律儀成就`) as the **result** rather than as a commitment made up front. The card ships the Chinese structure: recognition → growth, not recognition → resolve → growth. Flagged because a reviewer working from the Pali will notice the missing "commit to restraint."
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `律儀成就` ships as "becomes steadier," not "attains restraint" or "perfects the discipline."** `saṃvara` has no plain English word that passes the no-glossary screen; "restraint" reads as self-denial to a lay reader, and "the discipline" reads monastic. "Steadier" keeps the settled-conduct sense in ordinary speech. Flagged as a real de-technicalising, and the term now has a termbase row so the choice is recorded rather than re-made per verse.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `功德` ships as "goodness," not "merit."** The termbase's `puñña` row gives 福德 / 作福 for merit; `功德` is the adjacent term and this card is deliberately **not** a merit card — Days 97, 100, 108 and 111 have that ground thoroughly covered, and "merit" would pull it there. "Grows in goodness" keeps the sense of accrued good without invoking the merit economy.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `罪` ships as "fault," not "sin" or "crime."** `罪` carries both in Chinese; "sin" imports a theistic frame and "crime" a legal one. "Fault" is what the card is actually about.
- **⚑ THE PALI PARALLEL'S SURROUNDINGS ARE HARSH AND ARE DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED.** Immediately before `^sn12-70-29-4`, SN 12.70 runs an extended simile of a bandit arrested, bound, paraded to a drumbeat and beheaded, and says going forth as a thief of the teachings "has a more painful and bitter result. And it even leads to the underworld" (`^sn12-70-28-2` … `^sn12-70-29-2`). **None of that is on the card**, the Chinese source at this anchor does not contain it, and it must never be pulled in for "completeness" — it would fail the warmth screen outright.
- **Do not import the confession formula.** `我昔愚癡、不善、無智…` is the wording the Buddha gives Susīma to recite; it is first-person, particular to his case (`盜密出家`, going forth as a thief of the teaching), and unusable on a card. **Excluded.**

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **owning-a-fault**, new tag.

**Dedupe — checked against the English line of every one of the 111 shipped cards, not just against `source_ref`.** Nearest three, and why each is a different teaching:
- **Day 86** (Toh 249, purification-of-misdeeds): *"Four things overcome the harm you have done: regret, doing good instead, the resolve not to repeat it, and taking refuge."* That is a **four-part method** for undoing harm already done. This card offers no method at all — it is a promise about what the single act of honest recognition sets in motion.
- **Day 98** (Dhp 76, welcoming-correction): turns on **another person** pointing the fault out. Here nobody else is in the frame; the seeing is your own, which is what the doubled `自` insists on.
- **Day 101** (AN 10.2, joy-from-a-clear-conscience): *"Joy springs up all by itself when you have nothing to regret"* — addressed to someone whose conscience is already clear. This is addressed to someone whose conscience is not, which is the opposite starting position and the reason the card consoles.

**Gate checks.** *Buddhavacana* — the block opens `佛告須深` ("the Buddha said to Susīma") and the shipped sentence is his own generalisation. *In scope* — Saṁyukta Āgama. *Traceable* — `^pT02p0097c1714`, verified by grep (unique in the corpus). *License* — CBETA **CC BY-NC-SA** (source); Sujato **CC0** (Pali parallel). *Real quote, kept whole* — one complete sentence, contiguous. *Length* — en 112, zh 41. *Fresh* — SĀ 345 has never been used, and no Patton pairing exists for it.

**Screens.** *Arrives already applied* ✓ — the `凡人…者` universal-conditional shape, the same grammar the calendar already ships at Days 30, 79, 99 and 108. *Lands on one read* ✓ — Susīma's story is not needed and nothing in the sentence points outside itself. *One idea in five words* ✓ — "owning a fault grows you." The three Chinese consequences are one arc of growth, not an enumeration. *Warm* ✓ — it meets someone in shame and forecasts only gain; nothing austere or admonishing, and it does not make practice sound hard. *Dharma, not general life advice* ✓ — `律儀` and `功德` are both doctrinal, and the subject is progress in the training. *No "Buddha" in the card* ✓. *Not sober* ✓ (given the exclusions above). *Not a proclamation of attainment* ✓.

**Batch spacing.** Distinct from Day 117 (mutual care) and Day 119 (where safety lives). ⚑ This card sits **between** those two deliberately: 117 and 119 both turn on guarding oneself and would collide if adjacent.

Recorded here because this card came from the first real sweep of that file.
