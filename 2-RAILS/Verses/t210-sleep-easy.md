## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded clause-by-clause on the verbatim Chinese source (`zh-dharmapada.md#^lgT04p0560c2201`):

1. **持戒者安** — "one who keeps **ethical conduct** (持戒 = *sīla*) is **at ease** (安)." The ethical person is settled and secure.
2. **令身無惱** — "it leaves the **body free of trouble** (無惱)" — no gnawing affliction or vexation.
3. **夜臥恬淡** — "at night one **lies down calm and untroubled** (恬淡)" — sleep is peaceful.
4. **寤則常歡** — "on **waking** (寤) one is **always glad** (常歡)."

The verse's single move: ethical living is what actually produces ease — a body without trouble, calm sleep, glad waking. It promises rather than commands.

**Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here):**
- **持戒 / 戒 ships as "ethics / live ethically"**, per `termbase.md` (*sīla* = ethics) and the Day 88 (`da-22`) precedent that 戒 ships as "ethics", not "keep the precepts" (reads monastic) or "morality" (reads preachy).
- **安** ships as **"rest easy / at ease"**, not "safe" (imports a danger frame the verse does not have).
- **無惱** ships as **"untroubled / free of trouble"** — 惱 is affliction/vexation broadly, NOT specifically "anger"; do not narrow it to "no anger".
- **恬淡** is an archaic compound (calm, unruffled, desireless); modernise to plain **"calm / peaceful"**, do not carry the classical word through.
- **寤** is an obsolete character for waking; modernise to **"waking / on waking"**.
- **常歡** ships as **"always glad"** — ordinary gladness, not "rapture / bliss".
