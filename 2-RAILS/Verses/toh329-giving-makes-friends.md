**The shipped verse, verbatim from that block:**
> གཏོང་བས་མཛའ་བཤེས་འཕེལ་བར་འགྱུར། །བྱམས་པས་དགྲ་རྣམས་ཞི་བར་བྱེད།
> *(verbatim substring of `bo-toh329.md#^p517`. The speech frame `བཅོམ་ལྡན་འདས་ཀྱིས་བཀའ་སྩལ་པ།` ("the Blessed One said") stands immediately before it. Nothing altered, nothing elided.)*

**⚑ This is two of the stanza's four padas, and the stop is deliberate.** The full quatrain reads:
> གཏོང་བས་མཛའ་བཤེས་འཕེལ་བར་འགྱུར། །བྱམས་པས་དགྲ་རྣམས་ཞི་བར་བྱེད། །ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས་ཀྱིས་ནི་མཐོ་རིས་ཐོབ། །ཤེས་པས་ཐར་པར་འགྲོ་བར་འགྱུར། །

The deity asked four separate questions in one stanza (what increases friends? what pacifies enemies? how are the higher realms reached? how does liberation happen?) and the Buddha answered them **one pada per question**. Padas 3 and 4 are therefore separate answers to separate questions, not the completion of a thought begun in padas 1 and 2 — which is what makes the stop a clause boundary rather than a truncation. Two further reasons not to carry them: pada 3 turns on `མཐོ་རིས` (the higher realms), which is cosmology the card cannot gloss, and the whole quatrain runs past the character budget in every language. **Precedent for stopping at a clause boundary inside a stanza:** the `toh189-faith-and-awakening` pool entry.

## Disambiguated Meaning
Two padas, grounded in the Tibetan source (`bo-toh329.md#^p517`), with 84000's English consulted as a meaning reference only:

1. **`གཏོང་བས་མཛའ་བཤེས་འཕེལ་བར་འགྱུར།`** — "by **giving** (`གཏོང་བ`), **friends** (`མཛའ་བཤེས`) **increase** (`འཕེལ་བར་འགྱུར`)."
2. **`བྱམས་པས་དགྲ་རྣམས་ཞི་བར་བྱེད།`** — "by **loving-kindness** (`བྱམས་པ`), **enemies** (`དགྲ་རྣམས`) are **pacified / made calm** (`ཞི་བར་བྱེད`)."

The claim is causal and behavioural: two things you can do, and what each one does to the people around you. It is not a promise about your inner state, and it is not an instruction to feel differently — it says that generosity grows the circle of people close to you, and that meeting hostility with loving-kindness takes the heat out of it. The two halves are one idea in two directions: **the people around you respond to how you treat them, on the friendly side and on the hostile side alike.**

- **⚑ The verse's own negative twin sits eight lines earlier and must not be imported.** In answer to the deity's previous question, the Buddha says `མི་ཤེས་པས་ནི་འཇིག་རྟེན་བསྒྲིབས། །གཏི་མུག་གིས་ནི་དབང་དུ་བྱས། །ཞེན་པས་མཛའ་བཤེས་འབྱེད་པར་བྱེད། །ཆགས་པས་མཐོ་རིས་འགྲོ་མི་འགྱུར། །` — greed and clinging *separate* friends. Our two padas are the positive answer to the positive question. The card must not carry the loss framing.
- **⚑ 84000 note [n11] is a terminology note on our first pada, and it matters for the rendering.** `གཏོང་བ` (Skt. *tyāga*) generally means "giving up," "sacrifice," or "liberality," and 84000 explicitly distinguishes it from `སྦྱིན་པ` (Skt. *dāna*), the bodhisattva perfection. **So this verse is not using the `dāna` term.** The `termbase.md` *dāna* row (giving/generosity · 布施 · `སྦྱིན་པ` · दान · दान · өглөг) is the right English/Chinese/Hindi/Nepali/Mongolian target for the *sense*, but the shipped `bo` line should **not** silently substitute `སྦྱིན་པ`, which would import a technical frame the Degé does not have. Flagged, not fixed - see the bo note in the card's QA.
- **⚑ No emendation in these two padas.** 84000's note [n12] (which the `toh329-merit-undestroyed` rail flags as a Degé emendation) attaches elsewhere in the sūtra, not here; note [n10] records only that this question-and-answer set is absent from the Chinese T592 while present in the Tibetan and both Sanskrit witnesses. Grounded on the Degé as it stands.
- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) `མཛའ་བཤེས` is "friends" in the ordinary sense — companions and well-wishers — and is **not** `དགེ་བའི་བཤེས་གཉེན` / *kalyāṇa-mitta*, the spiritual friend of Day 80. Do not let the English drift toward "spiritual friends." (b) `བྱམས་པ` is the locked *mettā* term and ships as **loving-kindness**, never "love" (`termbase.md`, and the standing paraphrase warning). (c) `དགྲ` is literally "enemy." ⚑ **This is the live register question on the card:** "enemy" is accurate but large for a reader whose actual situation is a difficult colleague or a family member who is not speaking to them. Options weighed: keep "enemies" (faithful, preserves the friends/enemies pairing the verse is built on) versus soften to "those who are set against you" (relatable, but wordier in every language and slightly editorialising). The card ships "enemies"; a reviewer may overrule. (d) `ཞི་བར་བྱེད` is causative — loving-kindness *makes them* calm — so an English passive ("enemies are pacified") loses the agency; the card keeps the transitive.

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **giving-and-kindness** - a new tag. Card type: **teaching** (Day 120 was reframe, Day 121 consolation, so no consecutive repeat).

**Nearest shipped card, and why this is a different teaching.** The nearest is **Day 103** (AN 5.161, *"Live with love for someone you resent"*). That card is about **what to do with your own resentment** - an inward instruction for the reader's state. This one is about **what your giving and your kindness do to the people around you** - an outward, social claim. The second-nearest is **Day 54 / Day 100** (generosity cards): those are about the spirit in which you give; this one is about what giving does to your relationships, and giving is only half of it. **Day 97 is Toh 329** - same sūtra, a different stanza, four folios apart, different theme; the precedent for two cards from one text is recorded in `log.md` (Days 102 and 107 from one Toh 313 block).
