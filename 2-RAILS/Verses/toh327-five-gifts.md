## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Tibetan source (`bo-toh327.md#^p508`) and the 84000 reference. Five conditions on the *manner* of giving, not on what is given:

1. **`དད་པས`** — **with trust / confidence** (*saddhā*). Giving because you actually believe in it, not for show or from pressure.
2. **`གུས་པར་བྱས་ཏེ`** — **respectfully**, with regard for the person receiving. The same `གུས་པ` that anchors `toh231-merit-with-respect` (Day 63).
3. **`རང་གི་ལག་པ་ནས`** — **with one's own hands**. Not delegated, not at a distance; you hand it over yourself.
4. **`དུས་སུ`** — **at the right time**, when it is actually needed rather than when it suits you.
5. **`གཞན་དག་ལ་མི་གནོད་པར`** — **without harming others**, so the gift does not come at someone else's cost.

The teaching's whole weight is that generosity is not measured by the size of the gift. Every one of the five is about *how*, and all five are ordinary actions a lay person can check themselves against today — which is what makes it a good daily card rather than a doctrinal list.

- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) `དད་པ` is *saddhā* — **trust / confidence**, not "faith" in a believing-doctrine sense; the everyday "with trust" is closer than a religious register. (b) `རང་གི་ལག་པ་ནས` is literal and should stay literal ("with your own hands") — it is the most concrete item and the one a reader will feel; abstracting it to "personally" loses that. (c) `དུས་སུ` is "at the right/proper time," not "on time" or "promptly." (d) This is a **five-item list and all five must ship** — dropping one to save characters would be a distortion, not a trim; the English fits at 120, so there is no pressure to. (e) The vocative and the "what are the five?" frame are dropped so the card reads as a description of a good person rather than a monastic catechism.

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **how-to-give** — a fresh tag, deliberately distinct from the two existing generosity cards: Day 13/54 `generosity` (that one should give) and Day 45 `non-attached-giving` (the spirit behind giving). This one is purely practical: the five things that make a gift well given. Selected on tone and concreteness after two earlier Tibetan candidates were rejected for this batch — Toh 315 for resting on **Brahmā**, which does not travel to East Asian or Tibetan readers, and Udānavarga 26's "unborn, unbecome, unmade, unconditioned" for being too abstract to land on a daily card (Evan, 2026-09). Every item here is an ordinary action. Gate checks: buddhavacana (the Buddha addressing the monks directly); one complete sentence containing all five items, quoted whole; intelligible with no backstory and no glossary term; concrete and actionable; not a proclamation of attainment. In scope (mdo). Length: en 120 chars — inside budget with all five items intact. License: Degé PD source; own rendering; 84000 reference only.
