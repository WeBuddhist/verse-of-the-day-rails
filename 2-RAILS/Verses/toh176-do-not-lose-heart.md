## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Degé Tibetan (`bo-toh176.md#^p457`), with 84000 consulted for meaning only:

**`ཞུམ་པའི་སེམས་མ་སྐྱེད་ཅིག`** — "**do not give rise to a `ཞུམ་པ` mind.**" `ཞུམ་པ` is to shrink, be cowed, lose heart, be daunted — the state of being deflated by something that has shown you your own smallness. `སྐྱེད་པ` is to generate or give rise to, so the instruction is precisely about not *letting the state arise*, not about suppressing it once there. `སེམས` is mind or heart (termbase: mind, heart · 心 · སེམས · मन / चित्त · मन · сэтгэл).

The whole content is one imperative addressed to a person who has just deflated. It offers no method and no argument: it is a hand on the shoulder.

- **⚑ THE SCENE IS WHAT MAKES THE READING, and it is excluded from the card.** Verified in the file: immediately before this sentence, the sūtra says the wisdom and eloquence of the Realized Ones is inconceivable, and **Ānanda responds that from now on he will no longer claim to be foremost in learning** (`བཅོམ་ལྡན་འདས་དེང་སླན་ཆད་བདག་མང་དུ་ཐོས་པའི་མཆོག་གོ་ཞེས་དམ་མི་འཆའོ།`). The Buddha's reply is this sentence. **So the deflation is specifically the deflation of comparison** — Ānanda has measured himself against something vast and given up his own standing. That is the felt state the card meets, and the rail records it because the card cannot.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `ཞུམ་པའི་སེམས་མ་སྐྱེད` ships as "Do not let your heart sink."** The literal "do not give rise to a discouraged mind" is stilted, and "do not be discouraged" (84000) is flat and slightly dismissive in English — it is what you say to someone whose feeling you are brushing past. **"Let your heart sink" is the English idiom for exactly this state**, it keeps `སྐྱེད་པ` ("let… arise") rather than commanding the feeling away, and it keeps `སེམས` as heart. Collocation screen: this is what a person would actually say.
- **⚑ The card is 27 characters, and that is a deliberate choice rather than a thin one.** It is the shortest card on the calendar after Day 115 (70) and the banked Toh 219 (44). The whole teaching is one imperative; padding it to a more usual length would mean adding content the source does not have. The unusual brevity also leaves generous room in the other five languages, where this kind of consolation often needs more words than English.
- **⚑ Do not import the continuation.** The sentence is immediately followed by `དེ་ཅིའི་ཕྱིར་ཞེ་ན།` ("and why is that?") and then a long comparison of Ānanda's learning with that of the bodhisattvas. **Excluded** — it is the argument, it is many sentences long, and on a card the bare reassurance is stronger than the reasoning behind it. It must not later be added as if the card had been truncated.
- **⚑ Vocative dropped, and it matters more here than usual.** With `ཀུན་དགའ་བོ` in place the line is addressed to Ānanda and the reader watches; without it, it is addressed to the reader. The `ཁྱོད` is kept precisely so the address survives the cut.

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **discouragement**, new tag.

**Dedupe — checked against the English line of all 114 shipped cards.** Nearest are **Day 116** (T389, *"If you keep up steady effort, nothing is hard; so keep at it, like a small stream…"*) and **Day 15** (Dhp 25, effort). **Both are about persistence** — keep going, output over time, the work will accumulate. This card is not about the workload at all: it is about **self-estimation**, the moment you compare yourself with someone and decide you are not enough. It addresses the feeling, not the effort. Also checked **Day 81** (joy in doing good), **Day 84** (radiance of practice) and **Day 101** (joy from a clear conscience) — all uplift by pointing to a result; this one does not point anywhere. **Nothing in 114 cards touches discouragement or self-comparison.**

**Screens.** *Arrives already applied* ✓✓ — a second-person imperative, and `ཁྱོད` is in the source. *Lands on one read* ✓ — no narrative, no referent, nothing to decode. *One idea in five words* ✓ — "do not lose heart." *Warm* ✓✓ — pure consolation; it is the Buddha catching someone mid-deflation, with no admonition and no mortality frame. *Dharma, not general life advice* — ⚑ **the screen to watch, and the rail's answer is the scene.** Stripped bare, "don't be discouraged" is something anyone might say.
