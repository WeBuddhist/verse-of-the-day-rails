> [frame] Cattārimāni… speech with five factors is well spoken … Kālena ca bhāsitā hoti, saccā ca bhāsitā hoti, saṇhā ca bhāsitā hoti, atthasaṁhitā ca bhāsitā hoti, mettacittena ca bhāsitā hoti. (`^an5-198-1-3`)

## Disambiguated Meaning
Five marks of speech that is well spoken (`subhāsita`): timely (`kālena`), true (`saccā`), gentle (`saṇhā`), beneficial/helpful (`atthasaṁhitā`), and spoken with a loving heart (`mettacittena`) (1-SOURCES/Translations/en-anguttara-nikaya-sujato.md#^an5-198-1-3). Not a rule against bad speech — a picture of good speech.

**⚑ The fifth factor `mettacittena` ships per the mettā termbase** ("with a loving heart" → 慈 / བྱམས་ / मैत्री), rendered as "kind/loving," never flattened to generic niceness.
