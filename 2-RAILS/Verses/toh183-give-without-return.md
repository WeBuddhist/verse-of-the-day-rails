## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Tibetan source (`bo-toh183.md#^p194`), with 84000's English consulted as a meaning reference only:

1. **`ལན་ལ་མི་རེ་བ་ལ་གནས་ཏེ`** — "**dwelling in** (`ལ་གནས་ཏེ`, resting in, standing in) **no hope of return** (`ལན་ལ་མི་རེ་བ`)." Not merely *without* expecting return — *established in* not expecting it. The construction makes the absence of expectation a place one stands rather than an accident of mood.
2. **`སྦྱིན་པ་བྱིན་ན`** — "if one gives a gift (`སྦྱིན་པ`)."
3. **`རྣམ་པར་སྨིན་པ … རབ་ཏུ་འཐོབ་པར་འགྱུར`** — "the **ripening** (`རྣམ་པར་སྨིན་པ`) that is obtained is…" — the fruit metaphor is explicit in the Tibetan and should survive.
4. **`བླ་ན་མེད་པ་ཡང་དག་པར་རྫོགས་པའི་བྱང་ཆུབ`** — "**unsurpassed** (`བླ་ན་མེད་པ`), **perfect, complete** (`ཡང་དག་པར་རྫོགས་པ`) **awakening** (`བྱང་ཆུབ`)." The standard formula for full buddhahood.

The teaching's move is to identify the one condition under which an ordinary act reaches the largest possible result. The whole sūtra is a list of gifts and their matching fruits — flowers yield flowers, a bell yields a sweet voice — and this is where it lands: the gift with no expectation attached yields awakening itself. Read alone, without the list, it says something simpler and still true: expect nothing back, and what grows is not small.

- **⚑ The sūtra's list structure is deliberately outside the card, and this matters.** Thirty-six other gift-and-fruit pairs precede this sentence. Two of them (`the gift of flowers`, `the gift of a bell`) were considered as cards and **held back** because a modern reader may hear them as transactional or magical — give object X, receive benefit Y. This sentence is the one that does **not** have that problem, because what it names is not an object but an attitude. **Do not restore the list context, and do not pair this card with the flower or bell verses.**
- **⚑ Register risk, stated plainly: "unsurpassed, perfect awakening" is the loftiest phrase in this batch.** It is the accurate rendering of the standard formula and it is what the source says. But it sits oddly against the plain register the other four cards in this batch use, and against the newcomer-facing screen — a reader with no background may glaze at it. **Options a reviewer should weigh:** keep it (accurate, and *bodhi* is pan-Buddhist vocabulary); reduce to "perfect awakening" (drops `བླ་ན་མེད་པ`, a real loss); or "the highest awakening there is" (plainer, same sense, slightly longer). The card ships the full formula; this is flagged as the single most likely thing a reviewer will want changed.
- **Rendering choices flagged (not fixed here).** (a) `བྱང་ཆུབ` ships as **"awakening,"** never "enlightenment" (Victorian, and not the vault's word) and never anything with "Great Vehicle" in it — `termbase.md`'s ecumenical rule is explicit, and the *bodhicitta* row locks "the awakening mind." (b) `རྣམ་པར་སྨིན་པ` should keep the **ripening/fruit** metaphor — "what ripens," not "the benefit" or "the reward," which would flatten it and reintroduce the reward-agent problem. (c) `ལན་ལ་མི་རེ་བ` is **no hope of anything in return** — not "selflessly" (abstract, and imports *anattā* which is not here) and not "generously" (which says nothing). (d) `ལ་གནས་ཏེ` ("dwelling in") is **not** rendered on the card, for length — the card says "with no hope of anything in return," losing the sense that this is a settled standpoint rather than a passing intention. Flagged as the card's one real omission.

## Theme & Selection Notes
Theme: **giving-without-return** — adjacent to **Day 45**'s `non-attached-giving` (T235 Diamond Sūtra, "a bodhisattva should give without dwelling in anything"), fifty-five cards back. The claims are close cousins and a curator should know it; this one is specifically about *expecting nothing back*, where Day 45 is about not fixing on the act.

Selected against all the screens on file:
- **Self-applying** ✓ and **no inference step** ✓ — an imperative to **you**, with its result stated. This is the screen that killed Dhp 228 in the same proposal.
- **One idea** ✓ — nameable in five words: *give expecting nothing back.*
- **Warm** ✓ — it promises rather than warns, and asks for nothing difficult.
- **Public-facing** — ✓ on content, ⚠ on register; see the lofty-phrase flag above. Nothing here would put a newcomer off in substance, but the wording might lose them.
- Not sober ✓ · no deity frame ✓ · not a proclamation of attainment ✓ · no named person, place or scene ✓.

Gate checks: **buddhavacana** — the whole sūtra is one unbroken Buddha discourse to the monks (`དེ་ནས་བཅོམ་ལྡན་འདས་ཀྱིས་དགེ་སློང་རྣམས་ལ་བཀའ་སྩལ་པ།`), closing `བཅོམ་ལྡན་འདས་ཀྱིས་དེ་སྐད་ཅེས་བཀའ་སྩལ་ནས།`; **no other voice speaks anywhere in the text**, which is unusually clean for this gate. **In scope** — Kangyur mdo, Mahāyāna. **Traceable** — `bo-toh183.md#^p194`. **License-cleared** — `vault-annex.md` corpus row 14 (PD Degé; 84000 English reference-only per §7). **Real quote, kept whole** — a contiguous sentence stopped at a clause boundary. **Intelligible out of context** ✓. **Length** — en 91 chars, the shortest of this batch. **Fresh** — Toh 183 has never been used.
