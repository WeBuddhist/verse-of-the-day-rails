## Disambiguated Meaning
Grounded in the Degé Tibetan (`bo-toh56.md#^p197`), with 84000 consulted for meaning only:

1. **`དུག་ལྡུད་མཚོན་གྱིས་འདེབས་པ་ཡི། །གཅིག་ལ་གཅིག་ནི་གནོད་བྱེད་པ།`** — "**those who harm one another, striking with poison and weapons**." `གཅིག་ལ་གཅིག` is the reciprocal "each to each," so the harm runs both ways: this is a quarrel, not a victim and an aggressor.
2. **`སེམས་ཅན་དེ་དག་བསྡུམ་པར་བྱ།`** — "**those beings are to be brought back together**." `བསྡུམ་པ` is specifically to reconcile, to make peace between parties. The gerundive `བྱ` has an implied agent, which on a card is the reader.
3. **`དེ་ནི་མཁས་པའི་མཚན་ཉིད་དོ།`** — "**that is the mark of the wise**." `མཚན་ཉིད` is a defining characteristic, not a compliment.

The teaching names a specific act — stepping into someone else's rift and repairing it — and identifies it as what wisdom actually looks like. The reader is **not** a party to the fight, which is what makes this different from every conflict card already on the calendar.

- **⚑ Rendering choice: the gerundive is shipped as an imperative.** `བསྡུམ་པར་བྱ` is "is to be reconciled," agentless. Rendered literally ("those beings are to be reconciled") the card becomes a passive statement about the world and fails the arrives-already-applied screen. "Bring them back together" supplies the reader as agent. Flagged as a real grammatical move, and it is the same class of decision the vault already discloses on the Nikāya cards where Sujato supplies a second person.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `དུག་ལྡུད་མཚོན` is kept, not softened.** "Poison and weapons" is vivid and violent, and the temptation is to reduce it to "who hurt one another." Keep it: the specificity is what makes the stanza concrete rather than an abstraction about harmony, and the violence is described as happening **between other people**, never threatened against the reader. That is why the card still passes the warmth screen — the closing line is praise, not warning.
- **⚑ Rendering choice: `མཁས་པ` ships as "the wise," not "the learned" or "the skilled."** 84000 has "the learned ones," which in English suggests scholarship; `མཁས་པ` covers both learning and skill, and on a card about acting well "the wise" is what an English speaker would say.
- **Do not import the neighbouring stanzas.** The immediately preceding stanza in the same block is `སེམས་ཅན་རྣམས་ནི་ཐམས་ཅད་ལ། །མཉམ་པ་ཡི་ནི་སེམས་སུ་བྱ།` ("toward all beings, make the mind of equality") — ⚑ **note this is the same content as Toh 269, which is Day 122 in this batch**; the two must not be merged or confused. The stanza after concerns bearing abuse and praising patience. Both **excluded**.

## Theme & Selection Notes
