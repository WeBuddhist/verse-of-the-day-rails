# Day 147 — Seeing Through Appearances (Toh 95, Lalitavistara)

Rail: [[2-RAILS/Verses/toh95-illusion]] · own modern rendering from the Degé Kangyur (Public Domain); 84000 English as reference only. Mahāyāna.
One quatrain, **quoted in full**. Meaning: appearances are dreamlike and empty; even the wheel of the teaching, set turning, is like an illusion.

> **⚑ Source is Tibetan — the shipped `bo` is a MODERN rendering, not the verbatim Degé verse.** Verbatim sits in `## Source`; modern `bo` back-translated against it in QA.

> **⚑ Two rendering decisions on this card (see rail).** (1) The verbatim subject is the **wheel of Dharma the Buddha turned**, described as illusion-like — *not* "the world." An earlier draft ("this whole turning world is like an illusion…") shifted that referent; the shipped line keeps the wheel-of-truth referent. Curator can revert to the "turning world" wording in one line if preferred despite the shift. (2) The honorific **"Guardian of the World"** (the Buddha, third person) is dropped per Evan's call; shipped line is agent-less.

> **Output languages: six** (en · zh · bo · hi · ne · mn). Ladakhi (`lbj`) not rendered.
