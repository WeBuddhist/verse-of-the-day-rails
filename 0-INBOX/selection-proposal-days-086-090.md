## Day 86 — 2026-09-17 · Toh 249 · purification-of-misdeeds

- **Canon:** Tibetan Kangyur (Mahāyāna, mdo) — *The Noble Mahāyāna Sūtra Teaching the Four Factors*

## Day 87 — 2026-09-18 · Udānavarga 10 (Toh 326) · confidence

> **Substituted at Evan's direction.** The original Day 87 pick was Udānavarga 5 / Dhp 212 ("grief is born of what we hold dear"), rejected on the **anti-love misreading risk** — a casual reader meeting it cold on a card may hear *"don't love anyone,"* which is not what *piya* means but is what the card would say to this audience. That verse is now a **`rejected.md` entry with a reason**, not a banked candidate: reviving it would need a rendering that makes the clinging/affection distinction on the card itself, which ~120 characters may not allow. **Generalise the lesson:** a verse whose surface reading tells the reader to withdraw affection fails, even when the doctrine is exact and the verse resolves.

- **Canon:** Tibetan Kangyur (Udānavarga, ch.10 "faith")
- **Source:** [[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-udanavarga.md#^p437]] · Pali parallel **Snp 1.10** (Āḷavakasutta), verified line-for-line at [[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-sutta-nipata.md#^snp1-10-7-1]]–`^snp1-10-7-4`, Sujato English at the same anchors (grounding: `parallel-pali`). Duplicated at SN 1.73 and SN 10.12.
- **Buddhavacana:** ✓ the Buddha answering the yakkha Āḷavaka's question in the Pali parallel; Udānavarga verse in the Tibetan.
- **Verbatim Tibetan (verified at `^p437`), full quatrain:**

> འདི་ན་སྐྱེས་བུའི་ནོར་མཆོག་དད་པ་སྟེ། །འདི་ན་ཆོས་ལ་ལེགས་སྤྱོད་བདེ་བ་འཐོབ། །བདེན་པ་ཁོ་ན་རོ་བྲོ་རྣམས་ཀྱི་ཕུལ། །ཤེས་རབ་ཀྱིས་འཚོ་འཚོ་བའི་མཆོག་ཅེས་གསུངས།

- **Pali (verified):** *Saddhīdha vittaṁ purisassa seṭṭhaṁ, / Dhammo suciṇṇo sukhamāvahāti; / Saccaṁ have sādutaraṁ rasānaṁ, / Paññājīviṁ jīvitamāhu seṭṭhaṁ.*
- **Proposed English (117 chars) — the whole quatrain, no half-verse needed:**

> Faith is a person's finest wealth; living the teaching brings joy; truth is the sweetest taste; a life of wisdom is best.

- **Notes:** the earlier plan was to quote only the first two lines to fit; a tighter rendering fits all four at 117 chars, so **the whole verse ships** and the half-quote question falls away. The closing attribution tag `ཅེས་གསུངས` ("so it is said") is dropped. Rendering of `དད་པ` / *saddhā* to be settled against `termbase.md` at rail time — "faith," "confidence," or "trust"; Day 71 tagged this theme `confidence`.
- **Why now:** Tibetan slot; four ordinary goods named in one breath, nothing technical, nothing requiring a glossary. `confidence` last ran Day 71 (2026-09-02), so no adjacency problem.

- **Buddhavacana:** the Buddha replying to the brahmin Soṇadaṇḍa. ✓
- **Verbatim Chinese (verified at `^pT01p0096b1608`):**

> 如人洗手，左右相須，左能淨右，右能淨左。此亦如是，有慧則有戒，有戒則有慧，戒能淨慧，慧能淨戒。

- **Proposed English (124 chars):**

> Hands wash each other, left cleaning right and right cleaning left. So too virtue purifies wisdom, and wisdom purifies virtue.

- **Why now:** Chinese slot; an ordinary household image doing the work of a doctrinal point, which is exactly the register that has tested well (cf. Day 82's five gifts). ## Day 90 — 2026-09-21 · EĀ 卷23 · middle-way

- **Canon:** Chinese Āgama (Ekottarika, 卷23 — the Buddha's own account of abandoning extreme asceticism)
- **Source:** [[1-SOURCES/Text/zh-ekottarika-agama.md#^pT02p0671b2906]]
- **Buddhavacana:** ✓ the Buddha recounting his own decisive thought to the monks, in his own voice ("爾時，我復作是念"). Same category as the Toh 95 hesitation-to-teach reflection.
- **Verbatim Chinese (verified at `^pT02p0671b2906`):**

> 不可以此羸劣之體，求於上尊之道，多少食精微之氣，長育身體

- **Proposed English (122 chars):**

> You cannot reach the highest path with a body worn down to weakness; take some nourishing food and let the body grow strong.

- **Notes:** a contiguous opening portion of the sentence, stopped before `氣力熾盛，然後得修行道` — allowed under §2 ("quoting the opening portion and stopping is allowed"). Keeping `多少食精微之氣，長育身體` is what turns the line from a diagnosis into a remedy, so it should not be trimmed further.
- **Why now:** Chinese slot; fills the long-standing `middle-way` partial gap (only SN 1.1 so far) with the historically central instance — the actual turning point of the Buddha's own path, after six years of austerity. And it is quietly kind: permission to stop punishing yourself in the name of practice, which very few daily cards say out loud.
