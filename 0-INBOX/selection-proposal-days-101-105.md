## Where these came from

I stopped mining the pools and opened the two sources nobody had looked at:

**The Pali prose Nikāyas — never scanned, and the hypothesis paid off.** `SCAN-STATUS.md` has had DN/MN/SN/AN marked unscanned since the vault started (~245,000 lines). I reasoned that their register — short declarative sentences the Buddha says *to* a listener — is exactly what your screens select for, and it is. **Six of the eight candidates below are Nikāya finds.** The Aṅguttara is by far the richest.

**Two findings worth keeping.** (1) **DN is a low-yield source for this format** — 14,349 English segments, 35 survived filtering, zero candidates. It is narrative and its best lines sit in scenes, exactly like the Kangyur Mahāyāna sūtras. (2) **Sujato's English is doing real work on the "addressed to you" screen** — in three of these the Pali is impersonal and he renders it second-person. That is what makes them shippable, and each rail will say so, because a native reviewer will ask.

**The Kangyur's 13–26 kB band gave 2 from 23 files.** Honest and low, as expected. Every dead file is logged with a reason.

### A · AN 10.2 — joy from a clear conscience · **my top pick**
`pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an10-2-1-6` · Sujato at the same anchor (CC0, direct grounding)
*Dhammatā esā, bhikkhave, yaṁ avippaṭisārissa pāmojjaṁ jāyati.* — Sujato: "It's only natural that joy springs up when you have no regrets."
One idea: *no regrets means natural joy.* Theme `joy-from-a-clear-conscience`. Addressed to **you**.
Zero-context takeaway: I don't have to manufacture happiness — living so I have nothing to regret produces it by itself.
The warmest single sentence in the Aṅguttara, and the shortest card here. ⚑ Only the first link ships; the chain continues into jhāna vocabulary which must not be included.

### B · AN 5.161 — meet the one who irritates you with kindness
`pi-anguttara-nikaya.md#^an5-161-1-3` · Sujato: "You should develop love for an individual you resent."
One idea: *love the person you resent.* Theme `love-for-someone-you-resent`. Addressed to **you**.
Zero-context takeaway: the person I can't stop being annoyed at is exactly who to be kind to today.
**This is the closest thing the canon has offered to the still-open `forgiveness` gap** — and unlike every previous candidate (DA 27, Toh 249) it needs **no confession scene**. ⚑ Cite AN 5.161, not 5.162 — the near-identical twin is Sāriputta-spoken and fails the buddhavacana gate.

### C · Toh 313 — not the past, not the future
`bo-toh313.md#^p326`, complete four-line stanza · Buddha to a monk who came to ask
འདས་ལ་རྗེས་སུ་ཕྲད་མི་བྱེད། །མ་འོངས་པ་ལ་རེ་བ་མེད། །འདས་པ་གང་ཡིན་དེ་འགགས་ཏེ། །མ་འོངས་དེ་ནི་མ་ཕྱིན་པའོ།
One idea: *neither the past nor future.* Theme `present-moment`. Addressed to **you**.
Zero-context takeaway: stop re-running what already happened and stop waiting on what hasn't — neither is here.
No image, no list, no glossary word, nothing sober. **The strongest Tibetan candidate found in two scans.** ⚑ Textual trap caught: 84000 **emended the Degé** here (`ཕྲད` "meet" → `འབྲང` "follow") per its note [n6]; we ground on what the Degé says. The readings converge, so the card is safe, and the rail will record it.

### D · SN 11.21 — let go of anger and sleep at ease
`pi-samyutta-nikaya.md#^sn11-21-3-1`–`^sn11-21-3-2` · the Buddha's reply verse
*Kodhaṁ chetvā sukhaṁ seti, kodhaṁ chetvā na socati.* — Sujato: "When anger's slain you sleep at ease. When anger's slain there is no sorrow."
One idea: *drop anger, rest easy.* Theme `letting-go-of-anger`. Addressed to **you**.
Fresh angle against Days 3/7/14/65/83 — this one is about what anger *costs you*, not about its wrongness. ⚑ "Slain" fails the collocation test you named — English doesn't slay anger — hence "let go of." Same verse appears at SN 1.71, 2.3 and 7.1, which is good evidence of canonical prominence.

### E · Toh 292 — a blessing, not a teaching · **the interesting one**
`bo-toh292.md#^p531`, complete stanza · the Buddha blessing the **lay merchants of Vaiśālī** — householders, i.e. our actual audience
མཚན་མོ་བདེ་ལེགས་ཉིན་བདེ་ལེགས། །ཉི་མའི་གུང་ལ་བདེ་ལེགས་ཤིང་། །ཁྱེད་ཅག་ཀུན་དུ་བདེ་ལེགས་ལ། །ཁྱེད་ལ་སྡིག་པ་མ་འབྱུང་ཤིག
One idea: *may you always be well.* Theme `blessing-of-wellbeing`.
Zero-context takeaway: the Buddha is wishing me well, all day and all night. I am being blessed, not instructed.
**⚑ Needs your decision, because it is a new kind of card.** Every card in 100 days has been a teaching. This is a benediction — it asks nothing and instructs nothing. That may be exactly right for a daily touchpoint, or it may not be what the product is for. ⚑ One rendering ambiguity for the reviewers: `སྡིག་པ་མ་འབྱུང་ཤིག` sits between "may no harm befall you" and "may you do no wrong"; the blessing genre supports the first.

### F · MN 96 — beauty is not what makes you good
`pi-majjhima-nikaya.md#^mn96-7-8` · the Buddha's own first person (`vadāmi`), to the brahmin Esukārī
Sujato: "I don't say that being very beautiful makes you a better or worse person."
One idea: *beauty does not measure worth.* Theme `worth-is-not-appearance`. Addressed to **you**, in the Buddha's voice.
Unusually direct for the canon, and it lands on something a modern reader carries daily. ⚑ The adjacent sentence is the **wealth** version (`^mn96-7-9`, 74 chars) — equally clean if you'd rather not lead with appearance. Say which.

### G · EĀ 卷4 — trouble nothing alive
`zh-ekottarika-agama.md#^pT02p0564a0501` · Buddha to the monks
不觸嬈一切眾生，不害一切眾生，不惱一切眾生
One idea: *disturb nothing that is alive.* Theme `harmlessness`.
Three clauses asserting one thing, so it passes the one-idea screen the way Day 88 did. ⚑ The source closes `是謂無放逸行` ("this is called non-negligence") — deliberately dropped, since shipping a definition makes the card about a label. The mild risk is that it reads as instruction rather than consolation.

### H · Toh 313 (alt) — may everything alive be happy
`bo-toh313.md#^p326`, same block as C
སེམས་ཅན་ཀུན་དང་སྲོག་ཆགས་ཀུན། །འབྱུང་པོ་ཀུན་དང་ཐམས་ཅད་ཀྱང་། །བདེ་བ་འབའ་ཞིག་གྲུབ་གྱུར་ཏེ། །ཐམས་ཅད་ཉོན་མོངས་མེད་པར་ཤོག
One idea: *may everything alive be happy.* Theme `loving-kindness` (last ran Day 10).
⚑ **Mutually exclusive with C** — same source block. Also a blessing rather than a teaching, like E.
