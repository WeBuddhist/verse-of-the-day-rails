## Day 146 — 2026-11-14 · Chinese (Ekottarika Āgama) · guarding the mind [meditation]
- **Source:** EĀ, `[[1-SOURCES/Text/zh-ekottarika-agama.md#^pT02p0563c1202]]` · pool `EA guard-the-mind`
- **Verbatim (zh):** 云何為無放逸行？所謂護心也。云何護心？於是，比丘！常守護心有漏、有漏法。
- **Proposed EN (118):** "What is diligence? It is guarding the mind. And how do you guard it? Watch over the mind wherever trouble can leak in."
- **theme:** guarding-the-mind · speaks_to: scattered, wanting a simple practice, unsure what "mindfulness" means
- **Dedupe:** ✓. Defines heedfulness *as* guarding the mind — a plain, practical mindfulness card.
- **⚑ Note:** 有漏 (āsava / where defilement leaks in) rendered plainly as "where trouble can leak in"; rail to fix the term. Modern zh needed (source is Chinese).

## Day 147 — 2026-11-15 · Tibetan Kangyur (Mahāyāna) · seeing through appearances [wisdom]
- **Source:** Toh 95 (Lalitavistara), `[[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh95.md#^p426]]` · pool `toh95-illusion-of-phenomena`
- **Verbatim (bo):** ཇི་ལྟར་སྒྱུ་མ་སྨིག་རྒྱུ་དང་། །རྨི་ལམ་བྲག་ཅ་ཆུ་ཟླ་དང་། །དེ་དག་འདྲ་བའི་འཁོར་ལོ་ནི། །འཇིག་རྟེན་མགོན་པོས་རབ་བསྐོར་ཏོ།
- **Proposed EN (121):** "Like an illusion, a mirage, a dream, an echo, the moon on water, so the Guardian of the World has set this wheel turning."
  - **Alt, foregrounding the similes (92):** "This whole turning world is like an illusion, a mirage, a dream, an echo, the moon on water."
- **theme:** seeing-through-appearances (emptiness, image-based) · speaks_to: craving, equanimity, gripped by what feels solid
- **Dedupe:** ✓. The classic five-similes image; fresh angle vs the introspective `innate-purity` cards.
- **⚑ Two flags:** (1) the epithet **"Guardian of the World"** is the Buddha referring to himself in the third person — borderline on the "not about the Buddha" screen; the **alt rendering** sidesteps it by foregrounding the similes. (2) Buddha-spoken (answering Maitreya), Mahāyāna — word ecumenically.

## Day 148 — 2026-11-16 · Chinese (Ekottarika Āgama) · loving-kindness as the antidote to anger [loving-kindness / wisdom]
- **Source:** EĀ, `[[1-SOURCES/Text/zh-ekottarika-agama.md#^pT02p0667c0811]]` · pool `ea-q three-poisons`
- **Verbatim (zh):** 極盛欲心，要當觀不淨之想，然後乃除。若瞋恚盛者，以慈心除之。愚癡之闇，以十二緣法然後除盡。
- **Proposed EN (114):** "For craving, look closely at the body; for anger, loving-kindness; for delusion, see how all things arise together."
- **theme:** three-poisons-and-remedies (fills a known gap) · speaks_to: angry, caught in wanting, confused
- **Dedupe:** ✓. Names **loving-kindness (慈心)** as the remedy for anger and dependent-arising for delusion — carries the loving-kindness theme inside a practical antidotes card.
- **⚑ Note:** 不淨之想 (asubha / contemplating the body's unloveliness) rendered gently as "look closely at the body"; flag for your call on how far to go. 慈心 ships as "loving-kindness" per termbase.

## Day 149 — 2026-11-17 · Tibetan (Udānavarga, Toh 326) · the wise shine out [wisdom]
- **Source:** Udānavarga ch.18 (Dhp 58–59 parallel), `bo-udanavarga.md` · pool `udv-unstained-lotus`
- **Verbatim (bo):** ཇི་ལྟར་ཕྱག་དར་ཕུང་པོ་དག །མི་གཙང་ཆུ་ནི་མེད་འདི་ལས། །པད་མ་ཁ་བྱེ་གཙང་བ་དང་། །དྲི་ཞིམ་ཡིད་འོང་རབ་སྐྱེས་ལྟར། །… (paired simile + explanation; ships the lotus image, compressed)
- **Proposed EN (113):** "As a fragrant lotus opens clean and lovely from a heap of refuse, so the wise shine out with clear understanding."
- **theme:** the-wise-shine / unstained-in-the-world · speaks_to: discouraged by your surroundings, hoping to grow past where you started
- **Dedupe:** ✓. The classic lotus-from-mud image; ground on the Pali Dhp 58–59 parallel.
- **⚑ Two flags:** (1) source has 8 lines (paired simile + gloss) — ships **compressed** to the lotus image; needs a compression pass at rail time. (2) the gloss names **ཉན་ཐོས (śrāvakas)** — render inclusively as "the wise," never a vehicle label.

## Day 150 — 2026-11-18 · Chinese (Ekottarika Āgama) · wisdom above all [wisdom]
- **Source:** EĀ, `[[1-SOURCES/Text/zh-ekottarika-agama.md#^pT02p0759c0703]]` · pool `ea-n wisdom-is-supreme`
- **Verbatim (zh):** 戒律之法者，世俗常數；三昧成就者，亦是世俗常數；神足飛行者，亦是世俗常數；智慧成就者，此是第一之義。
- **Proposed EN (104):** "Good conduct, deep meditation, even wonder-working, all are ordinary; wisdom alone is the highest thing."
- **theme:** wise-priorities · speaks_to: chasing experiences/powers, wondering what practice is really for
- **Dedupe:** ✓.
- **⚑ Note:** 神足飛行 (psychic powers) rendered "wonder-working"; check tone — the point is that wisdom crowns the path, **not** that ethics/meditation are worthless; keep it as "these are ordinary, wisdom is what matters most," not dismissive.
