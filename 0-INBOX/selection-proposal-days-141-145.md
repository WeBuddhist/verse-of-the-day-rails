## Day 141 — 2026-11-09 · Chinese Āgama · a-little-is-enough
- **Source:** SĀ 908 (卷32, Asibandhakaputta), `[[1-SOURCES/Text/zh-samyukta-agama.md#^pT02p0231b1801]]` · pool `sa-908-one-line-understood`
- **Verbatim (zh):** 彼若於我說一句法，知其義者，亦得長夜安隱樂住。
- **Proposed EN (112):** "Whoever understands the meaning of even one line I have spoken will be at ease and glad for a long time to come."
  - **⚑ First-person screen:** contains "I have spoken." Recommend shipping the depersonalized form to clear the SĀ-963 first-person screen — **EN (119):** "Whoever understands the meaning of even a single line of the teaching will be at ease and glad for a long time to come." Curator call.
- **speaks_to:** overwhelmed by how much there is to learn · feeling you have barely started · doubting a small practice counts
- **Dedupe:** ✓ not in log/rails/days. Nearest is Day 12 (Dhp 100) — different claim (that speaks to the *speaker*; this speaks to the *listener*). The SĀ-963 sufficiency-ground collision is gone (963 was declined, Day 121 became Toh 176).
- **Grounding:** SN 42.7 (Desanāsutta) parallel.

- **Proposed EN (56):** "Never do to another what you would not want done to you." (drops the "I say this" frame; with frame = 68)
- **speaks_to:** angry at someone · about to retaliate · unsure how to act

- **Note:** instructs rather than gladdens (warmth screen); rail should carry the preceding Degé "everyone wishes to be happy" sentence as context (no stitching).

## Day 144 — 2026-11-12 · Tibetan Kangyur · wishing-abundance-for-all (aspiration)

- **Verbatim (bo):** སེམས་ཅན་ཐམས་ཅད་ལག་ན་རིན་པོ་ཆེ་དང་ལྡན་པར་གྱུར་ཅིག
